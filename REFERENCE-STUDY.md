- **Source not found.** The annex points to "the Austin annex / REFERENCES-ALL". No such file exists in this container; I searched the filesystem and the bank index.

- **What I build in the meantime,** working only from the annex's own words ("the MERSI seam mechanism" engineering "the transformation centrepiece" for the 4 genuine pairs):
  1. **The seam is the subject.** The join between before and after is a designed construction seam (a drawn joint line with a measured label), not a generic slider bar.
  2. **The seam carries a carrier.** The companion holds the seam handle.
  3. **The seam travels on its own once.** On first view it scrubs across with the scroll (as a scroll-scrubbed reveal), then hands control to drag.
  4. **Keyboard:** `role="slider"` with arrow steps (S1018).
  5. **Only the 4 genuine pairs** are presented as Elbaz work.
