# mood.md: Austin Pro brand mood (REBRAND 2026-10-31)

Reference file for critique-brand-consistency ⟦S1496⟧. Written from what the firm actually is: an Austin exterior
remodeler since 2009, James Hardie Elite Preferred and GAF Master Elite, whose proof is a documented record (886 jobs,
177 before-and-after pairs, 312 mapped jobs with photos) and 4.9 stars on 474 Google reviews. Nothing here is
aspiration the record cannot back.

## Personality keywords
- **Documented**: the record does the persuading. Figures, places, months, photographs.
- **Calm**: a home exterior is a large, slow decision. The page lowers the temperature; it never shouts.
- **Precise**: drawn to scale, measured, labelled. Lap courses, exposures, price bands as published.
- **Local**: Austin streets, Central Texas light, the firm's own job photos.

## Colour feel
- Light-dominant. Page grounds are the tinted neutral ramp (neutral-1/2), cards are white. Most of every page rests in
  neutrals ⟦S0071⟧.
- One accent, a deep teal (OKLCH hue 185): the colour of weathered copper flashing and of a painted front door. It
  appears where the visitor acts (the one filled button per view), where something is selected or focused, and in a
  few key figures. Never as a field behind body text, never on every card ⟦S1435⟧.
- No black or near-black grounds. The deepest surface is the neutral panel (neutral-3); the deepest colour is
  teal-12, used only for text on a teal tint.

## Imagery and illustration
- The firm's own job photographs carry the colour of the page (blue sky, green yards, painted siding). The UI stays
  neutral so the photographs are not fighting it.
- Line drawings (street elevation, gable, lap courses) are single-weight neutral line art, drawn to the trade's real
  dimensions. No gradients, glows, glass or blobs.

## Logo
- The course-gable mark: five lap courses cut at roof pitch; their ends imply the gable and a doorway gap reads as
  the A and a door ⟦S1422⟧. Accent on light, white on the accent, black or white where colour is not possible.

## Do
- Let one teal action own each view; give everything else a neutral outline.
- Show the measured thing: scale bars, exposures, counts with their source.

## Don't
- Saturated fills section after section, coloured headings, coloured eyebrows, rainbow charts.
- Decorative motion that delays or moves what the visitor is about to click.
