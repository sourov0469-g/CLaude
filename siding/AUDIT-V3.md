# AUDIT-V3 — homepage, 10 sections × 3 versions, under the reference method

Method (restart brief §3–4, BANK-MAP "How to use"): every version must trace to a named reference in the bank or the skills
library, with the mechanism copied and then adapted to Austin Pro's facts, photos and brand. Versions in a section must be
wholly different designs (layout, composition, style). A version composed from instinct, or a re-skin of a sibling, fails
and is rebuilt from a reference picked via the BANK-MAP section playbooks → Atlas → bank source → live reference.

Sources read for this audit: `bank-index/BANK-MAP.md` (front matter, rules, playbooks 1–10, mechanism families, the
roofing/siding manifest chunk, siding reports chunk, usage log), Atlas entries for every reference named below,
`previews/bank-selection/siding-windows-doors/structure-uiux/builder-brief.md` §2–6 (recipes R1–R45, F17 motion ceiling,
pin rules, F18 cost lab, standing standards) and `portfolio-site-plan.md` (Site 6, Siding & Windows: crowns, steal-stack).
Live references opened and captured on 29 Oct 2026 (1280 px, full page): strongholdroofing.com, 4feldco.com, aha.com.au,
tequila.ae, kbfdesigngallery.com, westshorehome.com/locations/charlotte-nc/.

Facts: `siding/FACTS.md` only. Bank entries supply mechanisms, never content claims.

- **M** F17 motion;
- **D** a different design from its siblings;
- **P** template feel;
- **3D** where earned.
