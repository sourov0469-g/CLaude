Priced window jobs by brand, with the lines and what the maker says about them.

    Texas-made windows, custom-built at NT's own Dallas-Fort Worth plant. The firm's go-to for vinyl, and the only aluminum line it installs. Questions Accordion

08Questions

Window questions, answered

How much does window replacement cost in Austin?

Across the 376 priced window jobs we documented from 2022-2026, the middle half landed between $5,000 and $25,000, and the median fell in the $10,000-$15,000 band. Your price depends on how many windows you replace and how big they are, the brand and line, and the color and glass you choose. The inspection and estimate are free.

Which window brands does Austin Pro install?

NT Windows, built in Texas, is on 238 of our 376 priced window jobs. Pella (31), Simonton (54) and Andersen (38) cover most of the rest, and we’re an Andersen Certified Contractor at the Premier level.

Vinyl, aluminum or fiberglass: what do Austin homes usually get?

Mostly vinyl. 214 of our 376 priced window jobs were vinyl, 64 were NT’s Twinsulator aluminum and 33 were Andersen’s Fibrex composite. Pella’s Impervia fiberglass is also available.

Should I replace windows and siding at the same time?

Often, yes. The siding was replaced on 36% of our priced window jobs. When we do both, the windows usually go in first, so the new siding and trim seal cleanly around each opening.

Are you actual window installers or a sales company?

We’re an exterior contractor, in business since 2009, at 7801 N Lamar Blvd in Austin. We measure, order and install the windows you choose, and we back the labor with a lifetime workmanship warranty.

How long does window replacement take?

Most of the window-only jobs in our files took two days on site. The windows themselves are built to order for your openings, so the order comes first; we’ll give you the schedule at your inspection.

What warranties come with new windows?

Each brand carries its own manufacturer warranty. Simonton’s DaylightMax, for example, has a transferable lifetime limited warranty on parts and labor, and Andersen covers the 100 Series 20 years on glass and 10 years on other parts. Austin Pro adds a lifetime workmanship warranty on labor.

Can I finance new windows?

Yes, on approved credit: 0% APR for 24 months, 4.99% APR for 60 months or 7.99% APR for 120 months. Ask about financing at your free inspection.
