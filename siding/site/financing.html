04Questions

Financing, asked and answered

Who provides the financing?

GreenSky, on approved credit; not everyone qualifies.

What are the published plans?

0% APR for 24 months, 4.99% APR for 60 months and 7.99% APR for 120 months.

What is the firm's own worked example?

$15,000 at 7.99% APR for 120 months is 120 payments of $181.91.

Can discounts stack?

Holiday Savings (on contracts signed through November 30, 2026), Hometown Heroes and pay in full: the firm's own example takes $30,000 with all three to $22,500. Pay in full is not available with financing.

What does a job cost?

Every trade has a published band from its priced jobs; the price for your house comes in writing after the free inspection.
