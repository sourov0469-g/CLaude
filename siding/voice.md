# voice.md: Austin Pro voice (REBRAND 2026-10-31)

Reference file for critique-brand-consistency ⟦S1496⟧. These are the rules the site's copy has followed since the owner
design note of 2026-10-30; written down here so the critique checks against a file instead of memory.

## Tone
- **Direct and plain.** Say what the firm does and what the record shows. Short sentences, active verbs.
- **Evidence first.** Every claim is a published figure, a credential the firm holds, or one of its own jobs, with
  where it comes from ("from 415 priced siding jobs", "4.9 on 474 Google reviews").
- **Helpful, not salesy.** CTAs name the next real step ("Book a free inspection", "Call (512) 550-0000"), never hype.

## Vocabulary
- Use the trade's words: lap siding, board and batten, exposure, ColorPlus, standing-seam, flashing, inspection.
- Prices are "published bands" and "medians", never "estimates" the firm did not publish; bands are never summed.
- The firm is "Austin Pro" in running copy; the full name is "Austin Pro Siding, Windows & Roofing".

## Forbidden
- Superlatives the record cannot back: best, #1, leading, award-winning (the awards are named instead).
- Invented numbers, invented reviews, invented people, invented phone numbers or addresses.
- Em dashes and stray en dashes (ranges use en dashes only between numbers or days).
- Filler: "unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "transform your home", "we're passionate about".

## Microcopy
- Errors say what to fix in one line, as the booking form does ("Add your first name so we know who to ask for.", "Add a phone number for the call back.").
- Buttons are verbs plus the object. Secondary actions are plain links or neutral buttons.
