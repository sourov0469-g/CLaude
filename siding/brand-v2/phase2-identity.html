07 · Backgrounds

Four full-section backgrounds

Each one is a whole section, shown with real content on it. Text on every background is measured against the brightest value the field can reach.

B1 · Course field · light

The wall face, at rest.

Courses in white and neutral-1, with a neutral-3 shadow under each butt. Static CSS. Text-primary on the darkest band: 14.76:1.

B2 · Steel field, ordered dither

Colour as content.

A Bayer 4×4 dither (S1372), drawn only between steel-9 and steel-12, so white text is at worst 5.96:1. The field drifts slowly and stops under reduced motion, or when off screen or the tab is hidden.

B3 · Section drawing · dark steel

How the wall goes together.

The wall drawn in section as a technical sheet: stud, sheathing, wrap, lapped boards, with dimension ticks. It sets up the wall-assembly chapter.

B4 · Evening field · close

Call when the light's still good.

Vertical light folds in steel over steel-12, with the glow pooling lower right (S1364). It is reserved for the last section and the footer. The fold brightness is capped so white text stays at or above 5.96:1.

09 · Motion

Motion tokens

--duration-instant50 msState ticks: checkbox, toggle.
--duration-fast100 msHover and press feedback.
--duration-normal200 msDefault transition: menu, accordion, focus.
--duration-moderate300 msPanels and the before/after seam settling.
--duration-deliberate600 msThe logo draw and the course line laying itself (once per view).
--ease-standardcubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)Things changing state in place.
--ease-entercubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)Things arriving (decelerate).
--ease-exitcubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)Things leaving (accelerate).
--ease-drawcubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)A line being laid: the logo and the course line.
--ease-settlecubic-bezier(0.34, 1.3, 0.5, 1)A board landing: small overshoot, never past 1.05 scale.

Principles:

  • One mover per view. In the header it is the logo draw; in a section it is the course line laying itself.
  • Physical. A board lands, a line is laid, a cut runs. No spins, pulses or floating.
  • Quick. UI feedback 100–200 ms; one authored moment per view up to 600 ms; the sum stays under 700 ms.
  • Composite only. transform, opacity and stroke-dashoffset.
  • Reduced motion. Every duration token is 0 ms at :root; final states render at once.

Nothing loops. Nothing animates on its own after it has arrived, except the B2 and B4 fields, which drift slowly and stop under reduced motion.

10 · Cursor companion

A companion that lays the course line

Book an estimate (512) 550-0000See the work on the map

Touch screen: there is no companion on touch. It appears only with a fine pointer.

A · Course tick

A 10 px steel square follows the pointer with a short lag (lerp 0.22 per frame). Over a link or button it stretches into a 3 px course line under the target and lays itself across the target's width (200 ms, --ease-draw). A press compresses it to 0.92. It explains the brand's thread on every hover.

B · Corner bracket

Four mitred corners, 16 px apart when idle. Over a target they open to the target's box plus 6 px (200 ms, --ease-enter), framing it as photos are framed. Clear, but it competes with the focus ring.

C · Lap chip

A single board in section (a wedge) trails the pointer and tilts with horizontal speed, capped at ±12°. Over a target it lands level with a small settle (--ease-settle). The most characterful, and the most distracting while reading.

Rules for all three. The system cursor is never hidden. The companion is pointer-events: none and aria-hidden, and moves by transform only. It is off when (pointer: coarse) matches. Under reduced motion it snaps, with no lag, tilt or stretch.

Pick: A. It is the only one whose behaviour is the brand's own drawing (the course line). B overlaps the focus ring's job. C pulls the eye while you read.

11 · Design dials

Set before any page is drawn (S0651)

Design variance 4 / 10

A structured grid with one asymmetric move per section (a split hero, an offset photo). The trade sells level and plumb, so the layout should look measured.

Motion intensity 5 / 10

One authored mover per view (the logo draw, the course line), plus the before/after seam and the map. No ambient motion except two slow background fields.

Visual density 4 / 10

Airy, with facts set plainly. Proof lives in the photos and the map, not in packed stat walls.

13 · Generation test

Six new things, from the documents alone

The test: make six new things using only BRAND.md and the tokens, and log every decision the documents didn't answer. Each gap is now a rule in BRAND.md.

Gaps the test found (all now closed in BRAND.md)

  1. Email signature: the brand document had no rule for a person's name and role. Added: Inter 700 name, Inter 400 role, no title case.
  2. Van: the document did not say where the course line sits on a vehicle. Added: at sill height, the full length, steel-9, and never crossing a door handle.
  3. Business card: no rule said which facts go on print. Added: phone, email and address only. Ratings and credentials are left off print because they date.
  4. Touch icon: the tile radius was undefined at 180 px. Added: radius-sm (2 px) on every tile, at every size.
  5. 404: nothing was missing. Voice, type and the course line covered it.
