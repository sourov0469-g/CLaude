# Austin Pro: brand guidelines, short drafts for the three options

These are three draft identities for **Austin Pro Siding, Windows & Roofing**, made for the owner's choice (OWNER-CHOICE-GATE, 2026-11-03). The owner may pick one whole or mix parts across them, e.g. "logo from 1, colours from 3". Only the picked direction is carried into Stage 3.

**Boards:**
- `siding/redo/branding/boards/board-c1.png`, `board-c2.png`, `board-c3.png`;
- the side-by-side sheet, `comparison.png`.

**What is shared by all three:**
- **Colour family:** black + white + steel, light-dominant (white or a near-white is the main ground).
- **Fonts:** normal everyday fonts only.
- **The Austin shape motifs:** cut siding piece, lap profile, batten strip, corner post, shake, drawn with real depth.
- **Locked facts on the samples:**
  - (512) 550-0000;
  - 4.9 · 474 Google reviews;
  - GAF Master Elite and James Hardie Elite Preferred;
  - since 2009;
  - 7801 N Lamar Blvd Suite C70, Austin TX 78752;
  - info@austinprosiding.com.
- **The hero lines** use the firm's published wording where it exists: "We replace by the full wall or the whole house. No patch repairs." and "One free inspection covers every trade on the house."

## Option 1: Plan Corner (drafted precision)

**The idea.** The outside corner of a sided wall, drawn from above as an architect would: two walls (core, wrap gap, siding skin) and the corner post covering their ends. The plan is turned 45°, so the corner becomes a roof peak with the post at its tip.

**The logo**
- **Lockup:** the mark left of "AUSTIN PRO" in Montserrat 800 caps (tracking +0.06 em), with the descriptor "SIDING · WINDOWS · ROOFING" in Inter 600 caps (tracking +0.24 em) beneath.
- **Two-tone version:** walls in Ink, post in Steel 700.
- **One-colour version:** all Ink, or all White on dark.
- **Small variant (under 33 px):** the wrap gap closes into one solid chevron, and the post keeps its gap.

**Clear space and minimum size**
- **Clear space:** keep X clear on every side, where **X is the corner post's width** in the mark as used.
- **Minimum size:**
  - lockup, 140 px wide on screen and 35 mm in print;
  - full mark, 33 px;
  - small variant, 16 px.

**Colours**

| Name | Hex | Role | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#14181C` | Type, mark, dark ground | 17.8:1 on white |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Dominant ground | |
| Paper | `#F4F6F7` | Second ground | |
| Steel 100 | `#E2E7EB` | Rules, fields | |
| Steel 300 | `#A9B4BE` | Drawn lines on dark only, never text | 2.1:1 |
| Steel 500 | `#677685` | Secondary text, drawn lines | 4.7:1 on white, AA for body text |
| Steel 700 | `#3B4652` | Corner post, buttons | 9.6:1; white on it 9.6:1 |

**Fonts:** Montserrat 800 / 700 for display and headlines; Inter 400 / 600 for text and labels.

**Motif system.** Technical line drawings: isometric pieces outlined in Steel 500 at 1.4 px, faces filled White / Paper / Steel 100. The background is a faint 40 px drafting grid, and the hero carries course lines.

**Where it works best.** Credibility-first pages: the pairs, proof and cost guides. It holds up small and in one colour (truck doors, yard signs, favicons).

**Risks.** The chevron can read as a rank stripe or a generic roofing logo without the post or the name. It is the coolest and least warm of the three.

**Do**
- Keep the post in Steel 700 when two colours are available.
- Use the small variant below 33 px.
- Keep drawings as linework.

**Do not**
- Rotate the mark.
- Separate the post from the walls.
- Fill the wrap gap at large sizes.
- Add a colour accent outside the steel family.
- Set the name in anything but Montserrat 800.

## Option 2: Grade Stamp (primed-board warmth)

**The idea.** A lumber mill stamps every board with its grade. Austin Pro stamps its work: a stadium-ended stamp frame holding AP and the lap profile in section (three wedges against a sheathing line, thin at the top, thick at the butt).

**The logo**
- **Lockup:** the stamp left of "Austin Pro" in Poppins 600 title case, with the descriptor in Lato 700 caps (tracking +0.24 em).
- **One colour:** Ink on Primed or White, or White on Ink and on photographs.
- **Small variant (under 33 px):** a filled stamp with AP knocked out; the profile is dropped.

**Clear space and minimum size**
- **Clear space:** X on every side, where **X is the stamp's frame thickness × 3**.
- **Minimum size:**
  - lockup, 150 px wide and 38 mm in print;
  - full stamp, 40 px;
  - small variant, 16 px.

**Colours**

| Name | Hex | Role | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#1C1B19` | Type, mark, dark ground | 15.2:1 on Primed |
| Primed | `#F3F1EB` | Dominant ground (the primed-board white) | |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, fields | |
| Steel 200 | `#D9D7D1` | Rules, fields | |
| Steel 400 | `#9B9993` | Drawn lines only, never text | 2.5:1 on Primed |
| Steel 600 | `#5F5F62` | Secondary text | 5.6:1 on Primed |
| Steel 800 | `#36373B` | Buttons, the stamp on photographs | White on it 11.9:1 |

**Fonts:** Poppins 600 / 700 for display and headlines; Lato 400 / 700 for text and labels.

**Motif system.** Primed relief: the pieces drawn as solid off-white forms with a soft cast shadow and a lit top edge, as if pressed out of the ground. Far pieces soften.

**Where it works best.** Warm, homeowner-facing pages: the about story, crew, reviews. The stamp prints well on job-site signs, shirts and paperwork, and can be rubber-stamped for real.

**Risks.** Stamps and badges are a familiar craft cliché. The lap profile reads as a serrated edge until it has been explained once. The AP monogram alone is not distinctive.

**Do**
- Keep the stamp level in lockups.
- Tilt it up to 6° only when it is used as a "stamped" mark on a photograph.
- Keep Primed as the main ground.

**Do not**
- Distress or texture the logo master.
- Add mill numbers or fake grades.
- Recolour the stamp outside the palette.
- Put the stamp over busy photo detail without the shade.

## Option 3: Whole House (the street view, as a system)

**The idea.** A house corner seen from the street: the corner post in the middle, and two sided walls running away in perspective, five lap courses each, their thin shadow lines converging. It draws the firm's own rule: "We replace by the full wall or the whole house. No patch repairs."

**The logo**
- **Lockup:** the mark left of "AUSTIN PRO" in Roboto 700 caps (tracking +0.01 em), with the descriptor in Roboto 500 caps (tracking +0.24 em).
- **Two-tone version:** walls in Black, post in Zinc 800 with a ground-coloured knock-out edge.
- **One colour:** all Black, or all White.
- **Small variant (under 33 px):** three courses per face, with a wider knock-out edge on the post.

**Clear space and minimum size**
- **Clear space:** X on every side, where **X is the corner post's width**.
- **Minimum size:**
  - lockup, 140 px wide and 35 mm in print;
  - full mark, 33 px;
  - small variant, 20 px recommended (it holds at 16 px as two blocks and a post).

**Colours (galvanised)**

| Name | Hex | Role | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black | `#0E0F11` | Type, mark, dark ground | 19.2:1 on white |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Dominant ground | |
| Zinc 50 | `#F1F3F4` | Second ground | |
| Zinc 200 | `#D2D8DC` | Fields, rules | |
| Zinc 400 | `#96A1AA` | Drawn lines only, never text | 2.6:1 |
| Zinc 600 | `#5B6670` | Secondary text | 5.9:1 on white |
| Zinc 800 | `#2D343B` | Colour fields, buttons, the hero band | White on it 12.6:1; Zinc 200 text on it 8.1:1 |

**Fonts:** Roboto 700 for display and headlines; Roboto 400 / 500 for text and labels. One family, as the system's discipline.

**Motif system.** Flat steel planes: pieces drawn as crisp faces in White / Zinc 200 / Zinc 400 (Zinc 800 for single feature pieces), each with a hard offset shadow. **Colour as content:** the zinc steps are shown as full fields, as on the hero's lower edge.

**Where it works best.** System-heavy pages: the visualiser, colour fields, the trade index. The perspective corner animates naturally: courses slide in from the post outward.

**Risks.**
- At 16 px the courses merge into two blocks, so 20 px or more is advised for favicons.
- Without the name, the corner can read as an open book or an office tower.
- It is the most "corporate" of the three.

**Do**
- Keep the courses' shadow lines thin (about 1/8 of a course).
- Keep the post's knock-out edge in the ground colour.
- Use the zinc steps as full fields.

**Do not**
- Flatten the perspective.
- Add a roof or chimney to the mark.
- Thicken the shadow lines (it becomes a ladder or a shutter).
- Set display type in any family other than Roboto.

## Generation test (all three)

Each board ends with four artefacts made from these rules alone:
- an 18 × 24 in yard sign;
- a truck door;
- a reversed business card;
- an app icon and a 16 px favicon.

They use only the lockup rules, colours and fonts above. No new decisions were needed to make them.

That is the test: an identity whose rules can produce new pieces without the designer.
