## 4. Hero - the owner's spec, final

- A TORN-APART home: on first view the home sits broken open / exploded into its pieces (siding courses, shakes, battens, fasteners, wall layers apart in space).
- It ASSEMBLES and fixes itself into the composed, finished home (the load animation IS the assembly).
- With the visitor's CURSOR: hovering / moving through the hero SCATTERS the pieces again with the fluid water-like push (Immersive Garden cursor behaviour); when the cursor MOVES OUT, the home FIXES ITSELF again, pieces returning to place on their own.
- Both hero versions (protocol, section 8) are wholly different executions of this torn-apart / assemble / scatter / self-fix world. The interaction must be flawless and light; the assembly replaces any loader and never blocks the headline for long.

## 8. Build method - one section at a time, deep, audited

- Homepage FIRST. ONE section at a time, and deep: a section is not done when it renders, it is done when it survives audit.
- Exactly 2 versions per section, wholly different, both at full quality - the owner wants depth per section, not breadth.
- After building each section: FRAME-BY-FRAME self-audit - scroll it slowly, hover and click everything, run the animations repeatedly, check 1440 and 390, hunt for jank, breaks, misalignments, dead interactions; FIX everything found and re-audit. Animation + interactivity is exactly where things break; assume they did until proven otherwise.
- Then: commit, screenshots at 1440 and 390, GATE-<section>.md with the scorecard (Design 40 / Usability 30 / Creativity 20 / Content 10; PASS = weighted at least 7.0, no category under 6.0, zero vetoes), WEB-SKILLS tags, defects found-and-fixed and remaining defects honestly named. STOP for Muse's judgment before the next section.
- Order: HERO, services, visualiser, windows, proof/reviews, process (full-view stacked chapters, NOT five cards), service area, CTA + footer, nav polish. Homepage 10-11 sections; interactivity sequence counts inside that.
- NO standalone before/after centrepiece: the hero carries the transformation story. Genuine job photographs may be requested (section 9) where a section truly needs them, presented full-view and plainly.
- Copy budgets: hero at most 40 words; headlines at most 8 words, support at most 35.
- Delivery: ONE single HTML file, all pages inside, in-file navigation. Other pages (5-6 sections minimum) follow the homepage's proven style after it is judged through. Superseded versions to hidden in-file backups, never deleted.
- Opus 5.5 + High only. NO sub-agents, NO background tasks: every step in this session's own thread.
