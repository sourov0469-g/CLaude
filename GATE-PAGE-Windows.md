# GATE-PAGE — WINDOWS (route `#/windows`)

**Under:** `siding/redo/final/PAGES-BRIEF.md` (inner pages), the homepage GLOBAL RULES and the owner's hard rules. **Files:** `parts_page_windows.py`, `src/page_windows.css`, `src/page_windows.js` (`window.FPWindows`), `tools/page_windows_test.js`, `tools/page_windows_shots.js`, shared `tools/page_test_lib.js`, `tools/pbuild.py`, `tools/pages_prep.py` (photo re-encode), screenshots `gate/page-windows-*`.
**Brief for this page:** a new invention, not the homepage's five-kinds configurator or its maker-count bar card; only published facts; no sums that are not published; no maps, no video, no outside links.

## 1. Self-audit and decisions

The homepage Windows section is one configurable window (kinds, colours, lock, time of day) and a bar card of the four maker counts. This page therefore avoids both and uses the firm's other window facts, each as a different kind of object:

| Published content | Object built | Why this and not the configurator |
|---|---|---|
| "Each opening is measured before anything is ordered … built to order" | **A tape you pull across an opening**; when it reaches the far side the window is set and the bead is drawn | The page's first promise is measuring, not choosing |
| Frame, glass, sill, finish per line (NT Executive 3-1/4″ multi-chambered vinyl, 3/4″ dual-pane glass, 99% UV, true slope sill; Twinsulator aluminum + thermal barrier; Presidential VividColor; Simonton slim frame; Andersen Fibrex; Pella Impervia fiberglass) | **An exploded unit you pull apart**, line by line, with numbered parts tied to a parts list | The published part facts are per line; a cutaway shows them, a configurator cannot |
| Low-E packages by maker | **A glass picker** with a sun you tilt and a Low-E switch (general section, flagged) | Four named packages, one drawing |
| Measure → order → built to order → set, sealed, old units hauled away; two days on site; the firm's own "What to expect" | **An ordering timeline you walk** (drag the marker, or arrow keys) through five scenes | The firm publishes the order of work, not a schedule |
| 376 priced jobs, 12 bands, medians by maker and city, same-job shares | **A price-distribution scrubber** with a maker flag, a city bar list and four share rings | Distribution, not a single number |
| Four published before/after pairs | **A before/after wipe** with tabs | The firm's own photos |
| Each maker's warranty + Austin Pro lifetime workmanship on labour | **Two stacked layers** (the maker's slides in on top of ours) | The warranty is two warranties |

**Honesty rules applied (and tested):** where the firm publishes nothing the page says so (Presidential glass package; Twinsulator colour names; NT Executive/Twinsulator warranty terms), no sum of the maker counts is printed (the firm's own "361 of 376" is deliberately not used), the line job counts are shown against each line only (89/54/33/49/33/2, never added), the glass drawing and the part geometry are labelled general, and Sara Helms's "about 6 weeks" is quoted verbatim as one customer's account, not a promised timeline.

## 2. What was built

1. **Opener, "Measured to the opening. Built to order."** Headline, the firm's own sentence, CTA to `#/contact` and the phone, four fact tiles (388 documented · 376 with a priced band, 2022–2026 · 2 days on site for most window-only jobs · lifetime workmanship warranty on labor). Right: an SVG wall and opening with a tape measure; a hook you drag (or move with arrow keys / Home / End); a dimension line appears when you reach the far side, then the window slides in and a sealant bead draws round it. It plays once on arrival (not in reduced motion), "Measure again" resets. Kit art: *try* (ruler and level) behind it.
2. **"Pull a window apart."** Six lines in a radiogroup, an oblique exploded drawing (finish, frame, glass pane(s), sill for the Executive only), a Pull-apart range and drag on the stage, numbered badges on the layers, a parts list (button per part; choosing one highlights its layer and dims the rest), published frame-colour swatches (Executive: Creme White and Almond; Simonton 10, Andersen 5, Pella 5, from `window_colours.json`; Twinsulator and Presidential say their colour names are not published), and the 376-job frame-material bar (214 / 64 / 33 / 4 / 61). Kit art: *windows* (light rays, ghost grid) inside the deep-green stage.
3. **"Most of a window is glass."** Four makers with their published package names; a sun you tilt (range or drag) and a Low-E coating switch that redraws transmitted and reflected rays in a general section. The card quotes the firm's sentence for each maker.
4. **"Windows are built to order, so the order comes first."** Five scenes (inspection with magnifier and clipboard, tape on the opening, a window being built at a bench with a "built to order" tag, old unit leaving and the new unit set and sealed with "most window-only jobs: 2 days", final check with a warranty sheet); a draggable marker on a five-stop track (keyboard: arrows, Home, End); the step card carries the firm's own wording.
5. **"Where 376 priced window jobs landed."** Twelve bars with the published counts (12/40/96/79/50/30/19/30/13/4/2/1), the middle half ($5k–$25k) bracketed, flags for the median band and the most common band, a scrubber (hover, drag, arrow keys), maker chips that add a median flag (NT, Simonton $10k–$15k; Andersen $20k–$25k; Pella, Provia $5k–$10k), the six-city bar list with each city's median band, and four share rings (36% siding, 22% exterior painting, 18% doors, 10% shingle roofing, with the "134 / 82 / 68 / 37 of 376" counts).
6. **"Same houses, new windows."** The four published pairs (Spicewood at Balcones Village Mar 2023, Ridgewood Jul 2023, North Austin Feb 2025, Maple Run Oct 2025) with their installed line and windows-portion band; a wipe you drag or key; eight real photos (720 px WebP).
7. **"Each maker's warranty, and ours on top."** NT Presidential VividColor 15 years · Simonton DaylightMax lifetime limited, parts and labor, transferable · Andersen 100 Series limited, 20 years glass / 10 years other parts · Pella Impervia limited lifetime, Pella covers labor 2 years; under each, Austin Pro's lifetime workmanship warranty on labor. Closing card: free inspection, `#/contact`, phone, hours, address.

**Background treatment (named):** each section sits on a dim sage ground (#F1F5F1 / #E8EFE9 gradients, never white) with one or two of the kit's line-art layers on parallax (try, windows, services, process, visualiser, reviews, services-near); the exploded stage is the one deep-green panel. No area/contour art anywhere (no map metaphor).

## 5. Defects found and fixed

1. The hook's dashed pulse ring sat below the drawing (positioned against the figure including its caption); the SVG and hook now share a wrapper.
2. The fixed header covered the opener's eyebrow; opener top padding raised; sections get `scroll-margin-top`.
3. Price-band flag stems ran through the count labels and the readout overlapped the bracket; stems are measured to the label, the readout moved above the bars.
4. Timeline marker sat off the node row and the scene blended into the card; marker and rail aligned, a sky and ground drawn.
5. Exploded layers overlapped too tightly and the frame and finish were the same white; gap and sizes changed, the structure layer is a darker sage.
6. A `Cannot access 'cn' before initialization` page error in the swatch row (a `const` used before its declaration).
7. The wipe's intro sweep overrode keyboard input (End did not stay at 100); the sweep is cancelled on any input. Timeline node buttons swallowed drags that started on them; a press on a button now becomes a drag after 6 px and a click is still a click.
8. Real contrast failures found by the test: the Low-E switch's "Off" in pale lime on a pale button (1.39) and the Fibrex frame segment in white on mid-green (3.06); both recoloured (lowest is now 5.47).
9. Booking CTAs used the homepage's internal `#booking`; they now use `#/contact`.
10. A build assertion compared "Exterior painting" case-sensitively against the firm's page; it is case-insensitive now. (The coordinator's note about this failure referred to the same line; the check passes.)

## 6. Remaining, named honestly

- **The unit is an illustration, not an NT or Andersen cross-section.** Geometry is general. Lines other than the Executive are drawn with a single glass pane because the firm publishes the pane count only for the Executive; the glass section shows a generic Low-E pane and says so on the page.
- **Unpublished things are not filled in:** the Presidential glass package, the Twinsulator colour names, NT's Executive/Twinsulator warranty terms, how long each step takes. The page states this instead of guessing.
- **Contrast is measured, not proven over art:** the test resolves text against the nearest opaque or translucent ancestor; kit art under text is ≤ 0.35 opacity line art and was not sampled pixel by pixel.
- **Testing:** Chromium only, software rendering; touch is emulated, not a physical phone. Isolated build (`PAGES_ONLY=windows` through `tools/pbuild.py`); the whole-site build was produced and loads, but the full-site test run was not repeated.
- **Integration note for the coordinator:** with `PAGES_ONLY`, the Contact page's `wire()` throws on its stub (`form` is null), which stops `FRoute.init()`; `tools/pbuild.py` wraps the other pages' `wire()` calls in try/catch for isolated tests.
