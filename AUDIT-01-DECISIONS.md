Every point of `AUDIT-PACK-01.md` is judged here before it is built: **ACCEPT** (built as written), **PARTIAL** (the good part built, my addition on top), **REJECT** (not built; replaced). Each ruling carries its reason and a catalog citation (⟦Sxxxx⟧ = `bank-index/atlas-part-1-skills.md`). Rulings are added in the order the work reaches them.

Ground rules I am working to:
- The pack's evidence folder (`~/workspace/previews/audit-elbaz/`, lane files, shots-01…06) is not present in this container. "Lane file 06 §4" (the full companion assignment table) is therefore unavailable; where the pack defers to it, I build from the pack text and say so.
- Facts outrank form (pack preamble, Part A.7): facts are fixed in every slot, the hidden backups included. A backup is the previous visible version kept reachable; a backup that prints an unlocked claim would still put that claim on the page.
