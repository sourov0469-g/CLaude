ROOFER LEAD COLLECTOR - START HERE
==================================
1. Install Python 3.12 (64-bit) from python.org. Tick "Add python.exe to PATH".
2. Double-click  0_SETUP_ONCE.bat   (one time, a few minutes, runs self-tests)
3. Double-click  1_OPEN_DASHBOARD.bat   -> your browser opens the dashboard
4. On the dashboard:
     1) Import your leads file
     2) Click "Test on the first 100" and look at the preview table (click a row for the full record)
     3) Click "Collect everything for every lead"   (stop and resume any time)
     4) Click "Download collected data (.xlsx)"  -> upload it to ChatGPT for your analysis

To stop everything: 2_STOP_EVERYTHING.bat.   Details: README.md
Your data stays on this PC (data\ folder). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
If the same website blocks the crawler ("Blocked by bot protection"), use "Retry sites that failed" later.
