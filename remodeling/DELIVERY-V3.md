## Systems

**Cursor companion** (bank: P10, Andrew Reff 381, S1303, S0489/S0792, 344, 239, Built Kindly 203)
- Variants: hard hat, spirit level, tape measure, or off. Choose in Settings (the panel bottom right on desktop). The choice persists.
- The system cursor stays visible; the companion trails it.
- It reacts differently to buttons, links, images, headings, cards, inputs and the drag slider. It blinks and rolls its eyes when idle, and does a small action now and then.
- It picks up each section's tool.
- It is disabled on touch and under reduced motion.

**Button reactions**
- Each button has its own drawn trade reaction; 31 are in use, among them blueprint roll, pencil box, hammer and nail, bolt, rubber stamp, tape blade, theodolite, screw, saw, pegboard hook, swatch fan, roller, storm shutter, chalk line, perforation, valve, level, viewfinder, trowel, masking tape, barrier tape, pushpin, work lamp, survey pin, stake, clipboard, door, brick, keys, handset and hard hat.
- The button itself never moves.

**Section fields:** one per section, all construction motifs, reacting to the pointer and to scroll.

**Dividers:** ten, all different (listed in PLAN-V3 §2.4).

## QA run on this file
- **Full scroll of every version at 1440 and 375, with motion and with reduced motion (12 runs):** no console errors, no element left hidden by an animation, no horizontal overflow, no em dashes in visible text. The one pin-spacer is hero v2's intended pin.
- **Switch cycle 1→2→3→1 on all ten sections at both widths:** no errors, no leftover pin spacers. ScrollTriggers do not grow; spent once-triggers retire.
- **Reactions:** every visible reaction was hover/leave-tested at 1440 and tap-tested at 375. None sticks.
- **Companion:** 147 targets across 3 variants × 30 versions, with no stuck states. It lands beside the pointer on first move, hides when the pointer leaves the window, and respects Off. On touch and under reduced motion it stays off and its switch is hidden.
- **Reviews:** all ten quotes are script-checked as verbatim against FACTS.md.
- **Forms:** the consult form validates inline, then composes the email (tested with the mail call intercepted).

## Flagged, not changed (with reasons)
- **F18 weight: 3.87 MB.** The one-file brief puts the photographs inside the HTML: 3.07 MB is the WebP image store (36 of the firm's own photos), fonts are 0.06 MB, and code is about 0.74 MB. This is the one F18 exception. A hosted build would lazy-load the photos and come in under 1 MB.
- **Section switcher (1·2·3) and the Settings controls are review tools, not site UI.** They keep a plain hover; on phones the sticky switcher can sit over a heading's last word. The same tool sat on the delivered file before, and it disappears once the owner picks versions.
- **Hero v3 extrusion:** the reference asks for a cursor tilt (7.5°). It was adapted so the face never rotates (bank rule: no rotated text-bearing elements); only the depth's vanishing side follows the pointer.
- **Proof v3 blur:** 4px instead of 5px, so the out-of-focus credentials stay legible.
- **Financing v3:** the reference's particle trail was dropped; the morph carries the moment.
- **Work v3:** the receding card scales to .9, not Lagom's .5, so the previous project stays readable as the next lands.
- **Images:** 36 of the 44 harvested photos are used. The five mosaic stills and two hero crops duplicate rooms already shown.
- **Not started, as instructed:** inner pages.
