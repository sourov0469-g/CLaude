## The eight-stage process (homepage, verbatim titles and week marks)
1. Consultation (Week 1): "We walk the property, talk through what you actually want to change, and give you an honest read on scope and budget before anyone quotes a number."
2. Site Evaluation (Week 1–2): flood zone, HVHZ requirements, permit history, HOA/historic review, what is behind the walls.
3. Design & Planning (Week 2–5): layouts, finishes and system choices, "the real trade-offs explained rather than one pre-set package."
4. Proposal & Scope (Week 5–6): "A written scope that says what is included, what is not, and where contingencies are likely. Priced against the drawings, not a guess."
5. Permitting (Week 6–12): permits pulled directly; every product checked for its Notice of Acceptance or Florida Product Approval.
6. Construction (Varies): one point of contact, inspections at each stage, "a site that gets cleaned at the end of the day."
7. Quality Review (Final 1–2 weeks): "We walk the work against the scope before we call it finished."
8. Completion & Handover (Closeout): final inspection, walkthrough together, registration of manufacturer and workmanship warranties.
Caveat line: "Timelines vary by scope and jurisdiction."
- Construction durations (homepage FAQ): "A single bathroom might run 4–8 weeks of construction; a kitchen often 6–12; a whole-home renovation several months." Permit review "can add weeks."
