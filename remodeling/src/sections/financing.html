Which partner fits your project?

Pick your project. The partners that usually fit it light up, with the reason underneath.

  • SynchronyRevolving Project Card. 6–132 months of special financing as published, one card reusable across projects.
  • Service FinanceFixed installment loan. First payment 30 days after completion, no pre-payment penalty. NMLS 140908.
  • YgreneFlorida PACE, repaid with property taxes. Up to a 20-year published residential term, soft credit pull.
  • Renew FinancialRenewPACE. Up to $250K published and up to 20% of property value, no application fee. NMLS 1788120.

Elbaz Construction is not a lender and does not approve, decline or fund applications. Every rate, term and approval decision comes from the financing partner. PACE covers specific improvements; a kitchen remodel is generally not one of them.
