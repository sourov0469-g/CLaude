Paying for it

Four financing partners, not one.

Permits and approvals

Straight answers on permits.

Who pulls the permits?

We do, in our own name. Elbaz Construction is a Florida Certified Building Contractor, licence CBC1265280. We keep one person accountable from the first walkthrough to final inspection.

Why does every product need checking here?

Miami-Dade and Broward sit inside Florida’s High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, where products need a valid Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance. We verify every product against its Notice of Acceptance before it is ordered.

Is Palm Beach County different?

Palm Beach is outside the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone but under Wind-Borne Debris Region rules.

How long does permitting take?

Our published permitting stage runs weeks 6–12, and permit review can add weeks. Timelines vary by scope and jurisdiction.

Can PACE pay for a roof or windows?

Roofing and windows and doors are among Ygrene’s published Florida PACE categories. PACE is repaid with property taxes; $0 down and 100% financing apply only where a PACE programme applies. Elbaz Construction is not a lender and does not approve, decline or fund applications.
