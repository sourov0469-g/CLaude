How a kitchen goes together

In a kitchen, the sequence is the job.

  1. 1LayoutA layout resolved before you are asked to choose a door style.
  2. 2Appliance scheduleWe will not quote a kitchen before the layout and appliance schedule exist, because a number without those is a guess.
  3. 3Cabinetry on orderThe cabinetry alone has a lead time.
  4. 4Plumbing and electricalThe plumbing and electrical behind the cabinets, handled as one scope.
  5. 5Cabinets, counters, finishLayout, cabinetry, counters and the services behind them, delivered as one kitchen.

A kitchen often runs 6–12 weeks of construction.
