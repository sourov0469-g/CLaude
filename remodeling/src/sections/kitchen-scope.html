Scope

Three kinds of kitchen job. We tell you which is yours.

  1. Refit scope

    Same layout, new kitchen.

  2. Full remodel

    Layout and services move.

  3. Open-plan scope

    The room itself changes.

What we build in a kitchen

  • Full Kitchen Remodel
  • Custom Cabinetry
  • Kitchen Cabinet Refacing & Replacement
  • Kitchen Countertop Installation
  • Kitchen Island Installation
  • Kitchen Layout & Design
  • Luxury Kitchen Remodel
  • Open-Concept Kitchen Remodel
  • Outdoor Kitchen Construction
  • Small & Galley Kitchen Remodel

What we will not tell you

  • We will not promise a building-department date.
  • We will not quote a kitchen before the layout and appliance schedule exist, because a number without those is a guess.
  • And we will not tell you a kitchen can be finished in a fortnight when the cabinetry alone has a lead time.
