  1. 01

    Consultation

    Week 1

    We walk the property, talk through what you actually want to change, and give you an honest read on scope and budget before anyone quotes a number.

  2. 02

    Site evaluation

    Weeks 1–2

    Flood zone, HVHZ requirements, permit history, HOA or historic review, and what is behind the walls.

  3. 03

    Design and planning

    Weeks 2–5

    Layouts, finishes and system choices, with the real trade-offs explained rather than one pre-set package.

  4. 04

    Proposal and scope

    Weeks 5–6

    A written scope that says what is included, what is not, and where contingencies are likely. Priced against the drawings, not a guess.

  5. 05

    Permitting

    Weeks 6–12

    Permits pulled directly, and every product checked for its Notice of Acceptance or Florida Product Approval.

  6. 06

    Construction

    Varies

    One point of contact, inspections at each stage, and a site that gets cleaned at the end of the day.

  7. 07

    Quality review

    Final 1–2 weeks

    We walk the work against the scope before we call it finished.

  8. 08

    Completion and handover

    Closeout

    Final inspection, a walkthrough together, and registration of manufacturer and workmanship warranties.
