# Mood: Elbaz Construction (rebrand 2026-10-31)

Source: the owner's verdict and the REBRAND DIRECTIVE annex (accent direction researched and owner-set).

**Personality keywords**
- warm
- built
- calm
- honest
- local (Miami)
- craftsmanlike

**Colour world**
- Fired clay, terracotta roof tile and stucco: the literal materials of South Florida homes.
- Earthy and muted: the accent is clay at low chroma, never a signal orange or a bright red.
- Neutrals are warm sand, not cool grey.

**Comfort first** (the owner's core complaint)
- Most of every view rests in neutrals.
- No saturated colour fields, no high-chroma fill behind body text, and no vibrating saturated pairs.
- The accent appears with discipline.

**Do**
- Light grounds.
- One filled clay action per view.
- Drawings in neutral line with one clay highlight.
- The firm's own photographs carry the colour of the work.

**Don't**
- Black or near-black grounds.
- Coloured section after section.
- An accent used for decoration, borders, labels or headings.
- Any hue outside the clay family.
- Moving characters that compete with the content.
