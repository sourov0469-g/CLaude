05 — Existing Practice

Community & philanthropy

Strength

Ramadan — PizzaBurg has provided pizzas to mosques for iftar, reported by Dhaka Tribune.

Christmas — employees dress as Santa Claus and distribute free pizzas to disadvantaged children, reported by Dhaka Tribune.

Reading: both fall under Carroll's philanthropic responsibility — voluntary contributions tied to a specific occasion.

Illustrative — Community Distribution
06 — Existing Practice

Workplace & environment

Strength

Workplace — PizzaBurg states a focus on a supportive, inclusive workplace, per a published CSR review.

Environment — a stated movement toward eco-friendly packaging and reduced plastic use.

Reading: these lean toward Carroll's ethical responsibility — beyond legal minimums, though not yet backed by published targets.

07 — Field Assessment

Strengths & gaps

Strength
  • Real, recurring goodwill — iftar giving and the Christmas giveaway are documented, repeated practices
  • Stated intent on employee welfare and environmental direction already in place
Gap
  • Visible activity is tied to calendar events — seasonal, not continuous
  • No public CSR report, published targets, or measured outcomes found
  • No evidence of a dedicated CSR budget, owner, or written strategy

08 — Suggestion 01 / 04
Recommended

Buy 1, Give 1

For selected meals, one purchase funds one donated meal.

MechanismA designated menu item triggers one donated meal to a partner shelter per purchase
CostSelf-funding — donation volume scales directly with sales
BenefitA concrete, always-on giving story, not one tied to a single occasion
Illustrative — Biodegradable Packaging
09 — Suggestion 02 / 04
Recommended

Green PizzaBurg

100% biodegradable packaging, with a box return/recycling incentive.

CostModerate upfront — packaging changeover plus incentive design
Market fitOver half of Bangladesh's population is under 25 — a segment that responds to visible sustainability moves
RegulationBangladesh has signaled a clear policy direction against single-use plastics — moving early positions PizzaBurg ahead of that trend
10 — Suggestion 03 / 04
Recommended

Student Support Program

Scholarships, free meals, or internships for financially disadvantaged university students.

CostLow, scalable — can start small and grow as results are demonstrated
PipelineInternship placements give early access to hires from the population PizzaBurg already recruits from
The caseEnlightened self-interest — the ethical action and the business action are largely the same action
Illustrative — Food Redistribution Partnership
11 — Suggestion 04 / 04
Recommended

Food Rescue Program

Donate unsold, still-safe food at day's end to shelters instead of discarding it.

CostNear-zero — this is food otherwise discarded, not new inventory purchased for donation
BenefitLower disposal costs, a strong reputational story, a small reduction in food waste
Operational noteRequires a basic food-safety handoff protocol — clear cut-off times and a documented chain of custody
