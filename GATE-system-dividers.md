# GATE: System · Dividers and Home seams

**Built:** ONE divider system, deepened (Muse ruling 1). Files: `remodeling/src/js/systems/fields.js`, `css/systems.css`.

## The rule applied (PHASE1-PLAN)
"Each divider is the ink line drawing a construction profile that belongs to the hand-off; dividers are part of the seams, not decoration."
- Five Home dividers did not belong to their hand-off: saw, truss, tile, shingle and level. They are replaced by profiles that carry the seam. Each is ONE ink line, drawn left to right with the scroll (scrubbed, reversible).

| boundary | new divider | what it carries |
|---|---|---|
| Process → Financing | **fork** | the process rail (eight marks) runs on, then forks into the two routes of the route finder |
| Financing → Projects | **rail** | the ink line becomes the picture rail; hooks drop in and the wires open as for the hung frames |
| Projects → Reviews | **tally** | gates of five rise in height left to right (the upward vector into the stars) |
| Reviews → Towns | **pins** | five pins on a dashed survey line: the five stars on their way to the map |
| Towns → Consult | **coast** | the coastline's wobble thins out into one straight ink line |

- **Kept:** dimension (Hero → Proof), beam, roofs, conduit and footing (Consult → Footer, with the footing the footer rises on).

## Measured
- **Scrub:** the fork divider's progress follows the scroll exactly: 0 → 0.14 → 0.41 → 0.70 → 1.00 as it rises from 105% to 50% of the viewport. Reversible.
- **One easing language:** seven overshoot eases (`back.out(n)`, `expo.out`) left in the kept dividers were harmonised to `power4.out`.
