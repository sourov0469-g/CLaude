## 1. Whole-page diagnosis

Read top to bottom as one experience, the homepage is a long document of explanations, not a place a homeowner moves through. The evidence:

**1. It is long, and the energy dies in the middle.**
- The shown default page is **19.2 screens** at 1440 (20.7 at 390).
- **Three sections take 12.6 of those screens (66%)**, and all three are scroll-driven sequences of drawings:
  - Services A, 3.3 screens: a sideways sequence of drawn boards;
  - Process A, 4.1 screens: four stacked chapters with outlined numerals and the same clipboard;
  - Try A, 5.2 screens: a pinned sideways set of drawn walls.
- From screen 7 to screen 16 the visitor scrolls through outlined "01 / 02 / 03 / 04" numerals and drawn walls (`upgrade-live-1440-a/b.jpg`, frames 07–16). Nothing in those ten screens answers cost, trust, their house, or booking.

**2. One scaffold, nine times.**
- Every shown section opens the same way: a left-aligned Montserrat 800 headline, a grey sub-paragraph, then a pale green-grey rounded panel holding one drawn object (board, window, clipboard, wall, tag line, work order). The panels in frames 01–18 are interchangeable.
- **116 of 126 audited versions** use the same headline shape: a short noun phrase ending in a full stop ("Pull a board.", "The index.", "Year by year.").
- Section after section, the reveal habit is identical too: split-word headline, then a stagger, then the panel.

**3. Drawings where the firm has photographs.**
- The firm has 43 published project photos and 177 published before-and-after pairs, yet **98 of 126 versions contain no photograph at all**.
- On the shown page, photographs appear only in the hero (on a canvas) and in Area (8). Services, Visualiser, Windows, Reviews, Process, Try and Booking show drawn houses, drawn walls and drawn windows. Across the archive: Visualiser 14/14 drawn, Reviews 14/14 text-only, Booking 13/13 without a photo.
- A homeowner asking "what could my house look like?" is shown a cartoon wall.

**4. The same figures, over and over.**
- On the shown page:
  - **19 "Book a free inspection"** buttons and **19 phone numbers**;
  - "documented" 21 times;
  - $10k–$15k six times;
  - 431 and 388 four times each.
- Repetition has turned the strongest numbers into wallpaper.

**5. The defaults were never re-chosen.**
- The shown defaults are all first-round versions: Hero **1**, Services A, Visualiser B, Windows A, Reviews B, Process A, Try A, Area A, Booking A.
- **Hero 1 is the version the switcher itself labels "backup, rejected photo".**
- 107 newer versions sit in the switcher, and the page the owner sees has never been assembled from the best of them as a whole.

**6. Judged one section at a time, scored on the same rubric.**
- The K–N gates scored 8.42–8.67, a band too narrow to separate anything, while the owner kept rejecting the result. Each version was compared with its own section's previous versions, never with the page around it.
- So the page gained versions, not rhythm. Two pinned sections sit back to back; two magazine treatments compete (Hero N, Windows N); MERSI's seam appears in five sections.

**7. The audience test** (cost · trust · their house · booking), section by section on the shown page:

| Shown section | Moves forward | Mostly explains |
|---|---|---|
| Hero 1 | booking (button, phone) | — |
| Services A | cost (bands) | 3.3 screens of drawn boards |
| Visualiser B | their house, as a drawn wall | the board-by-board build |
| Windows A | — | a drawn window through a day |
| Reviews B | trust, as small type on tags | — |
| Process A | — | 4.1 screens of steps |
| Try A | — | 5.2 screens of mini-games |
| Area A | trust (real jobs, real towns) | — |
| Booking A | booking (a form-like order sheet) | — |

Five of nine are explaining, not selling.

**What the upgrade must therefore do, page-wide** (not a global effect; rules every section build obeys):

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| P1. Photographs lead | The firm's real job photos carry Hero, Services, Visualiser, Windows and Area. Drawings are used only where they explain something a photo cannot (Process, Try). |
| P2. Ten screens, one pinned moment | The page target is **≤ 10 screens at 1440** (from 19.2). Each section ≤ 1.3 screens; **at most one** scroll-pinned section on the page. |
| P3. No two neighbours share a kind | The rhythm map in section 3 assigns each section a different kind, density and ground: full-bleed photo, tight interactive, quiet type, photo grid, and so on. No two adjacent sections share a scaffold, headline shape or reveal. |
| P4. Each number once, where it decides | 431 where siding is chosen; 388 where windows are; $10k–$15k at the cost moment; 4.9 / 474 at trust; 30-year at the warranty. "Book a free inspection" ≤ 6 times on the page (hero, cost, visualiser end, booking, header, mobile bar). |
| P5. Headlines in the homeowner's voice | At least half the section headlines are the homeowner's own question or claim, not a noun phrase with a full stop. |
| P6. A live unit per section | Each new section carries one bespoke animated unit of its own (custom SVG or photo interaction, small depth on one element), never a page-wide effect. |
| P7. The page is the unit | After the sections are built, one whole-page gate chooses the shown defaults together and checks rhythm, length and repetition across the page. |
