# Owl Roofing — Real Photographic Hero Pack

> Web-sourced concept photography for a portfolio build — illustrative use only, never caption as a specific Owl Roofing job.

All images below were visually checked by Muse: genuine camera photographs of real houses, roofs and roofers. Rejected during sourcing: anything AI-looking or rendered (including one crew shot installing panels over battens that read as generated), watermarked MLS previews, a Florida tile-roof house, and a portrait stock shot. Resolution note: only 3 of 10 reach 1600px+ wide — most roofer/MLS CDNs cap delivery size; the sub-1600 files are still the sharpest real versions publicly served and are flagged per file.
