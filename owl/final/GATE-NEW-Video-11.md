# GATE-NEW · Video 11 · "Press the Owl" (first of five new Video versions, homeowner-simple pass)

## Plan line (written before building)
- **The one homeowner sentence:** *See the real crew who'll be on your roof — press play.*
- **Primary governing reference:** Apple AirPods Pro (one full-width moment, one control, nothing else on screen to decode).
- **Style family:** a single ambient film band with one giant character button.
- **Signature move:**
  - The play button is an Owl's face. Its pupils follow the pointer, or the last tap on a phone, and it blinks now and then. Its beak is the play triangle.
  - Hovering makes the eyes widen. Pressing it opens the full crew film on YouTube (ehKLv5DF2TQ).
  - Behind it, Owl's own crew film loops silently.
- **How it differs from Video V1–V10, and why it is better:**
  - V1–V10 frame the film in players, lenses, windows, letters, story cards and projectors.
  - V11 removes all framing: the film is the background, and the only thing to do is press the Owl. That is the shortest path from "who are these people?" to watching them.
- **Rejected before building:** an Owl-branded truck with the film on its side. The branch has no evidence of an Owl vehicle, and the only truck photo on file belongs to another company, so it would invent a fact.
- **Skill ledger, per element:**

  - Its blink: ⟦S0489⟧.
  - Its beak as the play triangle: ⟦S0405⟧.
  - Full-film modal: ⟦S1046⟧.

## What was built
- **Header:** the kick, then the title **Meet the Crew Who'll Be on Your Roof**, then one line: "Real people, not stock footage. Press the Owl to watch them at work."
- **The band:** one full-width band of Owl's own crew film, looping silently and only while in view.
- **The Owl button:**
  - In the middle sits one big round Owl face: ink head, cream eyes, amber irises. Its beak is the amber play triangle, and a small "Watch the crew" tag sits under it.
  - Its pupils follow the pointer around the section, or the last tap on a phone, and it blinks every few seconds.
  - Hovering makes the eyes widen and the button lift.
  - Pressing it opens the full crew film on YouTube.
- **Under the band:** "Owl's own crew, at work. Want them on your roof?" and one button, **Get My Roof Checked**.

## Self-check (the owner's five questions), answered from my own screenshots at 1440, 768 and 390
1. **What is it for?** "See the real crew who'll be on your roof; press play."
2. **Do I get it in under three seconds, without reading?** Yes. The moving crew film plus a big friendly play button: everyone knows what a play button does, and this one is the Owl.
3. **Is it interesting, playful or fun? What exactly makes it so?**
   - The play button is a character. It watches your pointer, its eyes widen when you come near, and it blinks.
   - Its beak being the play icon is a small, satisfying visual joke.
4. **Is every element here for a clear reason?**
   - Title: who these people are to you.
   - Lede: they're real.
   - Film: the proof.
   - Owl button: the one action.
   - "Get My Roof Checked": the next step.
   - Things I questioned:
     - **The "Watch the crew" tag under the Owl:** kept, because without it some visitors might not read the Owl as a play button. The beak triangle alone is subtle at a glance.
     - **Chapter captions, a scrubber and a sound toggle:** all deliberately left out.
   - Nothing pointless remains.
5. **Does it fit a stressed homeowner?** Yes.
   - It answers "who'd be on my roof?" with real faces, in one look.
   - It offers the booking button right under the film.
   - The motion is calm: a silent loop, eyes that follow.
   - With reduced motion it shows just the poster and a still Owl.

**Verdict: passes.**

## Defects found and fixed
- None found after the first build; the screenshots matched the plan.
- **Rejected at the plan stage:** the Owl-truck idea. The only truck photo on file is another company's vehicle, so a branded Owl truck would invent a fact.

**Remaining, named honestly:**
- On phones the Owl button covers part of one crew member in the frame. The loop moves, so no face stays hidden.
- The Owl face is a simple flat drawing in the brand's ink and amber, not the painted Owl from the hero. That is deliberate, to read as a button.

## Reference transfer
- **Transferred from Apple AirPods Pro:** one moment, one control, the product (the crew) full-bleed.
- **Not transferred:** the scroll-scrubbed sequences and the dark staging.

## Compared with the section's previous best
- The owner liked a couple of the earlier Video versions. They frame the film in a player (V1) or a scene (V6 window, V9 projector).
- V11 keeps the same real film and the same full-film button, but makes the button the star and the film the backdrop. It is the fastest of the eleven to understand.
