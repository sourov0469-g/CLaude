Storyboard B · Sweep the lawn (the game, the peak)

Bruno Simon's mechanism, one explorable toy section, scaled to a lawn. You drag Owl's magnet across a drawn yard and find the nails hidden in the grass. Twelve are hidden; the count is the game's own, not a statistic. It is keyboard playable (arrow keys move the magnet), works by touch, and under reduced motion becomes a tap-to-reveal grid.
