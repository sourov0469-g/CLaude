Chapter 01

We've seen it all.

"From wind damage that's lifted shingles to hail that's left its mark." When a storm hits, Owl does three things, in this order.

  1. 01

    Assess the situation

    A thorough, professional inspection, "documenting every bit of damage with detailed reports and clear photos."

  2. 02

    Secure your home

    "We respond quickly to assess the situation, secure your home from further damage."

  3. 03

    Make a clear plan

    "A clear plan to get things back to normal", with an estimate that is "free, accurate, and designed to let you make an educated decision."
