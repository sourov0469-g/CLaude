Chapter 01 · Our story

"More in common than their due dates."

"Sometimes, life is all about timing. Tim Brown and Bea Bonte were in a class for new parents when they overheard Noah and Anya Bergland making roofing jokes and realized that they had more in common than their due dates."

"With both families working in the trades, they formed a close friendship, sharing the highs and lows of the industry (and parenting) with each other. So, when Noah decided to start his own roofing company, asking the Brown family to join him was a no-brainer."

Owl Roofing was born in 2025, family-owned and based in Shoreview.
