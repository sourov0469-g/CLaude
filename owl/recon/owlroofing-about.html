Our Story

Birds of A Feather Roof Together

Sometimes, life is all about timing. Tim Brown and Bea Bonte were in a class for new parents when they overheard Noah and Anya Bergland making roofing jokes and realized that they had more in common than their due dates. With both families working in the trades, they formed a close friendship, sharing the highs and lows of the industry (and parenting) with each other. So, when Noah decided to start his own roofing company, asking the Brown family to join him was a no-brainer. Using their combined experience to create a white-glove roofing experience like no other, Owl Roofing was born.

Meet The Browns!

Tim Brown and Bea Bonte are the founders and co-owners of Hook Agency, a Google-specialized marketing team serving the home services industry. After almost a decade working with some of the top roofing companies in the industry, they’ve learned a lot about what makes a successful business and a 5-star customer experience. Today, they use all of that hard-won knowledge to help Owl Roofing’s clients feel confident in the team that they’ve chosen.  

Meet The Berglands!

Noah and Anya Bergland have built their careers in the construction industry. Noah has worked in both the roofing and construction industries as a builder and project manager, while Anya has served as the lead content manager for home contracting companies. Put them together, and you have a dynamite team! As the founders of Owl Roofing, they’re committed to providing each and every customer with consistent quality and above-and-beyond service.

How much will a new roof cost?

Roof costs depend on materials, roof size and complexity, and any needed repairs. Owl Roofing offers a free inspection and detailed estimate so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you offer warranties on your roofing work?

Yes, we provide both a 10 year workmanship warranty on our installation and 50 year manufacturer warranties on all roofing materials. During your consultation, we’ll explain all coverage details so you know your roof is fully protected.

How long will it take to replace my roof?

Most residential roof replacements take one to three days, depending on weather, roof size, and any needed repairs. We’ll give you a clear timeline up front and keep you updated throughout the project.
