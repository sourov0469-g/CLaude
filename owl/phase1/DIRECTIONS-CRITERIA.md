# Phase 1b: the question and the selection criteria
Written BEFORE any direction was drawn (⟦S1472⟧ concept-selection: criteria first, or they describe the option already
preferred).

## The question the set answers (⟦S1474⟧)
> A Twin Cities homeowner lands on owlroofing.com. What do they DO first, and what carries them from the first viewport to
> calling or texting Owl?

The three directions differ in behaviour, not in looks. All three wear Owl's kept identity: the logo, the ten live
colours, the Montserrat/Lato stand-ins.

| | Direction | Entry point | Unit of interaction | Commitment point |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **The Crew** (people first) | Watch Owl's own crew film | Chapters of real moments | Meet who answers, then call |
| B | **The Nest** (system first) | Follow one line through the Protect Your Nest system | Pinned beats, one proof each | After the guarantee beat, call or text |
| C | **The Instrument** (price first) | Operate "Price My Roof" | Hands-on input | The measured roof hands off to a quote |

## Fixtures (identical in every direction; facts from the addendum's lock only)
- **Contact:** Owl Roofing LLC; 1000 County Rd E #230, Shoreview, MN 55126; (651) 977-6027; Mon-Sat 8-6.
- **Promises:** "We actually answer the phone"; "We Give a Hoot".
- **Credentials:** 5.0 · 90+ reviews (as Owl states it); GAF Systems Plus Certified; BBB Accredited; MN licence
  #BC809662; family-owned since 2025.
- **Systems:** the Protect Your Nest system (Nest-Protection wrap, 3-Part Cleanup with crew, magnet and project-manager
  passes, Owl Roof Aftercare / the shingle tomb); 10-year Get It Right Guarantee; up to 50-year manufacturer warranties.
- **Service area:** 29 towns.

## Criteria
**Thresholds** (fail one and the direction is out):
- **T1** The logo artwork and Owl's colours are untouched; no new identity.
- **T2** Only locked facts. No invented numbers, prices, reviews or job details.
- **T3** The hero video is designed into the hero (poster, load behaviour, fallback), not pasted behind text.
- **T4** It reads as the annex reference class, not a template or a document (⟦S0659⟧ identifiability test).
- **T5** The conversion floor holds: the phone and text action are reachable in every view, and a sticky bar exists on
  mobile.

**Trade-offs** (weighed):
- **W1** Distinctiveness: could a stranger name the business from a lone screenshot?
- **W2** How much of Owl's OWN material it puts to work (video, families, nest photos, badges, YouTube).
- **W3** Feasibility at depth tonight: 10-11 homepage sections × 2 versions, each with audit cycles.
- **W4** Performance and accessibility risk (pinning, video, 3D, reduced motion).
- **W5** Room for the required set pieces: a custom scroll animation per signature section (Graza bar), one game-like
  section (Bruno), the flow line (Lusion), and the cursor companion.
