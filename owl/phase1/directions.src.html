Three design directions on Owl's kept identity

Rebuild, not rebrand. Every frame wears Owl's own logo (unchanged), Owl's ten live colours, and the Montserrat/Lato stand-ins from Phase 1a. The directions differ in what the visitor does, not in how Owl looks (⟦S0330⟧, ⟦S1474⟧). The fixtures are identical in all three and every fact comes from the lock. Footage and photographs are Owl's own.

The question: a Twin Cities homeowner lands here. What do they do first, and what carries them to calling or texting Owl?
