Brand argument

Five short answers. No marketing prose.

What it represents
A family-owned Shoreview roofer that answers the phone and leaves the yard cleaner than it found it.
Core metaphor
The nest: Owl protects the home the way an owl guards its nest. Owl already calls its system "Protect Your Nest".
How the logo expresses it
The owl sits inside the O and watches, amber eyes open. The rebuild keeps it and lets it blink and glance in place.
How it scales
Page: the sweep line and the drawn pieces. Favicon: Owl's own JPG. Truck door or shirt: the white lockup on olive, as Owl's crew already wears it. Print: one colour, ink.
Why it is ownable
The line is Owl's 3-Part Cleanup, with the magnet pass included. No other roofer can claim the same drawing without claiming the same process.

Three adjectives

Watchful. Neighbourly. Tidy.

Shape language

Monoline strokes with round caps, the same weight family as Owl's wordmark counters. Wedges and courses come from shingles; one loose curve comes from the sweep. Corners: 6px on fields and 10px on media, as Owl's live site already uses; pills for actions. Nothing glossy, no glass, no gradient fills, no 3D.

Illustration style (⟦S1421⟧)Geometric-organic hybrid. Flat. Moderately detailed: a nail has a head, a shank and a point, and nothing more. Representational: real tools and real roof parts. Colour is a subset of the palette: ink lines, olive lawn, tan warmth, amber only where the magnet works.

The kept logo: variants, grounds and life

Owl's two files are injected byte-for-byte; only class names are added so that the existing eye and pupil paths can move. The artwork is never redrawn.

Contrast pairs, measured

Generated from the hex values by gen-tokens.py and printed from contrast-pairs.csv. A pair not listed here does not get used. Audit record: a derived third light ground ("moss") failed with amber-700 (4.38) and, rendered, could not be told from cream. The step was removed rather than nudged. amber-700 is derived, hue held, against cream, the darker light ground.

Type: Montserrat and Lato, on a generated fluid scale

Stand-ins for Owl's own Special Gothic Expanded and Texta, which cannot be used here (everyday-fonts rule; Texta is commercial). The scale uses the Utopia method: poles 360→1240px, base 18→20, ratio 1.2→1.25, with a rem term in every clamp and max ≤ 2.5× min. Weights loaded: 700 and 800 display, 400 and 700 body; none are synthesised. Five sizes in regular use: display, title, heading, body, small.

Motion tokens

Every duration and curve is a token, and similar elements use the same one. User-initiated motion stays under 300ms; staggers are 40ms; everything is interruptible. Reduced motion sets every duration to 0 and shows final frames.

--dur-instant 80
--dur-fast 160
--dur-normal 240
--dur-slow 480
--dur-story 900
Curves --ease-out (.23,1,.32,1): entering, responding, arriving. --ease-travel (.77,0,.175,1): moving on screen, gaze, morphs. --ease-in (.55,0,1,.45): leaving only. Never on something the user waits for. linear: continuous pans and wheels. Signature timings Blink 110 / 40 / 190ms. Glance: 480ms glide (--dur-slow), then a 1.2s hold. Press: 0.97 scale in 100ms, back in 220ms. Pinned beats: at most 4 per page, each at most 100vh of scroll. Frequency rule (⟦S0489⟧) Navigation and buttons get minimal, fast changes. Motifs play once per visit. Full delight (the game, the nest) is kept to places people pass rarely.

Offset, not chaos: asymmetric splits, varied media ratios and the line breaking the grid. A strict single column below 768px.

Scroll-scrubbed motifs and pinned beats, but calm curves and no idle wobble on chrome.

Visual density
4

Airy enough that one proof fills a screen; never a cockpit.

Cursor companion: the native cursor plus one companion

The native cursor is never hidden, so the hotspot is always exact. One companion trails beside it with frame-rate-independent lag, so a fast flick never makes it jump. It turns off on touch, under reduced motion, and when you choose Off.

Owl eyesThe logo's own eye motif (two amber irises on ink), not a redraw of the logo. The pupils look where you are heading and settle on the centre of a button. Over a link the eyes widen; a click blinks in the same frame; it never wobbles idly.
