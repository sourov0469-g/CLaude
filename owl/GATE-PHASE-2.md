## Defects found and fixed in this gate
1. **Runaway loop:** a loop in `bg.lawn` stalled page load with "Invalid string length". The course gap shrank
   geometrically and never reached the top. Fixed with a floor on the gap.
2. **The moss ground** was indistinct and failed with amber-700. Removed rather than nudged.
3. **Magnet pass final frame:** at p = 1 the stage was empty, because the sweeper had left with every nail. Under reduced
   motion that is the only frame shown. It now parks on stage with its nails.
4. **The nest** read as a flat bundle. Redrawn as a bowl in perspective (back rim, crossing strands, front rim, one amber
   strand).
5. **B3 night trees** read pink. Replaced with olive depths; the background no longer paints its own ground.
6. **Footer:** muted text used the light-ground olive on night (about 2.3:1). It now uses `--text-on-deep-muted`, and
   the treeline moved below the copy.
7. **Image-frame ticks** sat under captions and collided between neighbouring images. The ratio moved onto the media,
   and one inspector's corner remains.
8. **768px header overflow:** the nav breakpoint moved to 1023px. A real menu panel was added; the toggle had opened
   nothing.
9. **Reduced motion** showed frame 0 of the motifs. Motifs now rest at p = 1.
10. **Exemplar on mobile:** the beat's copy overflowed the pinned screen and hid the magnet stage. On mobile it now shows
    a compact headline and the current step only, with the stage placed above the call bar.
11. **The exemplar's flow line** ran through the "3" numeral and later through the video caption. It is now computed
    from the layout and tested against every text box in the hero: 0 collisions.
12. **Skip links** leaked into view. A visually hidden until-focused `.skip-link` component was added.
13. **Generation-test round 1:** 3 asset defects (a missing place-list component, an unassigned background, clipped ring
    symbols). Fixed in the assets; round 2 needed 0 inventions.
14. **Token lint:** off-ladder drift distances and board-chrome values were snapped to the ladder. JS press and glance
    timings now read their tokens.
15. **Process incident:** the harness moved one long-running render command to the background (the runaway loop above
    made it slow). Within seconds of seeing it, I stopped it with TaskStop and confirmed no browser or render process
    was left running.
    - **Cause:** that call had no explicit long timeout.
    - **Fix:** every render since has run in the foreground with an explicit timeout.
    - **A second slip:** `pkill -f` matched my own shell, as earlier tonight; from here I kill only by PID.
