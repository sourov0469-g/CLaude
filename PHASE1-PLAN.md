# Phase 1 plan: flow maps, storyboards, Financing concept, logo story, companion plan

## Governing rules
- **Authorities:** this plan applies the addendum, the brief, annex v3 and the owner's shortlist, together with Muse's Phase 0 ruling and Muse's structural rule.
- **Section counts:** Home has 10 to 11 sections; every other page has at least 5 to 6. Page count stays at 5.
- **Versions:**
  - TWO versions only where a choice exists.
  - One deepened winner where the owner already chose.
  - System parts (footer, dividers, logo, companion) stay at one each, deepened.
- **Craft rules:** these come from `docs/skills/notes-*.md`. Where a section reports a skill tag, the tag points into those notes.

## The site-wide flow law (the Lusion mechanism, applied with S0544/S0578/S0576)
- **The current** is downward (native scroll).
- **Reserved vectors:**
  - **Horizontal:** the build journeys only (Process, Bath build). Horizontal is a chapter boundary.
  - **Upward:** a reveal that rises (the Reviews tally).
  - **Z-pull** (shrinking exit, then an oversized arrival retracting): the Consult arrival.
- **The carrier is the INK LINE.**
  - **What it is:** one construction pen line (the site motif, ink colour, 1.5px).
  - **How it travels:** it leaves each section as part of that section's drawing and arrives as the frame or rule of the next. It is never a crossfade.
  - **Its other forms:** inside sections it is the dimension line, the seam and the progress rail.
- **Transition vocabulary** (three, repeated):
  1. **ink-line hand-off** (the default seam);
  2. **frame dock** (a container shrinks and docks into the next layout);
  3. **pin and release** (pinned scenes).
- **No hard cuts and no idle wobble.** Every pinned scene releases before the next section.
- **One easing language:** `--ez-out`, `--ez-move` and `--ez-in` (`notes-C`).

## Signature storyboards

### Process: 8 frames, one continuous drawn site (Home 5)
- **Mechanics:**
  - The stage pins at full view.
  - Horizontal track, 8 frames, scroll weight about 1 viewport height per frame (S1328 range).
  - `scrub:1`, `ease:"none"`, then release.
  - The camera pans across ONE long drawing (Immersive). Each frame's art animates while it is in view, using `containerAnimation` triggers.
  - A week mark lands as a numeral event on the rail (AirPods).
  - Labels run (01) to (08) (OH).
- **Frames** (stage titles and week marks verbatim):

| frame | stage | what is drawn |

## Financing: two fresh concepts (none of the old ones is reused)
**A. The route finder.** A drawn site plan with a fork in the road.
- **Project kind:** the visitor picks one, with a "not sure" option always present:
  - Interior: kitchen, bath, flooring.
  - Exterior and energy: roof, impact windows, HVAC, storm protection.
- **Routing, by published fit:**
  - Interior routes to Synchrony (revolving, "6–132 months" published by Synchrony) and Service Finance Company (fixed installment; first payment "30 days after completion"; "0 pre-payment penalty").
  - Exterior and energy also routes to Ygrene and RenewPACE (property-assessed; "20 year maximum published residential term"; "$250K published maximum, and up to 20% of property value"). The firm's own line "a kitchen remodel is generally not one of them" stays visible.
- **Partner cards:** each figure lands as an event and is attributed to its lender.
- **Beside the CTA:** "Elbaz Construction is not a lender and does not approve, decline or fund applications."

**B. Two kinds of money.**
- **Ledgers:** two drawn columns, "consumer credit" and "property-assessed", in the firm's own framing: "Two are consumer credit and two are property-assessed".
- **Comparison:** scroll scrubs a comparison line by line through how you repay (revolving, fixed installments, property tax bill), with each published figure as an event.
- **No calculators and no invented numbers.**

## Logo: the story, "a course laid, a room closed"
- **The mark:** a bracket (the E's spine and arms, open on the right) plus a clay tile set in the gap (S1422 closure). The micro-story is told only through these two shapes, and only on a cause, never idly (S0544).
- **Beats:**
  1. **Load:** the bracket draws like a pen (top arm, then spine, then bottom arm; `pathLength` 0 to 1 over 0.5s with `--ez-move`). The tile drops into its course with a 1px settle squash. It tells the founding of a course.
  2. **Header compaction on scroll:** the arms shorten and the tile tucks closer. The micro shrink and expand keeps the closure readable at small size.
  3. **Hover:** the tile slides along its course to touch the spine and complete the E, like a trowel tap, then slides back on a slower, softer hover-out.
  4. **Press (go home):** the bracket closes into a full square for one beat (the room is closed), then opens again.
  5. **Page switch:** the tile is the shared element across the five in-file pages (view transitions, S1276).
  6. **Footer:** the closing beat. The full square holds as the page ends.
- **Reduced motion:** a static mark with the closure intact.

## Companion: rebuild plan (Phase 2)
**Diagnosis** (render at `scratchpad/buddy-hat.png`, code `js/systems/buddy.js`):
- The hat group `.bd-hat` is a sibling of `.bd-head`, with its own y tweens (including `bounce.out`). So it drifts off the head and floats above the face with a visible gap. That is the owner's bug.
- The face is small and plain.
- The per-section TOOLS and the react.js/rig.js sprites add a third cursor thing, which the owner bans.

**Rebuild:**
1. **Structure:**
   - The hat lives inside the head group, anchored at the brow (the brim overlaps the forehead by 2 units), and pivots with the head (S0357 identity lock).
   - The face gets a brow line, expressive eyes (pupils follow the pointer) and a mouth set: smile, "o", flat, grin.
2. **The rule of two:** the pointer tip plus the companion, and nothing else. Remove `react.js`, `rig.js` and the TOOLS sprites. Variety comes from the companion's own reaction per element kind.
3. **Physics follow:**
   - a spring at about 2.2 Hz, ζ about 0.72, mass 1.2;
   - lean from velocity, ±10°;
   - interruptible;
   - one rAF loop that stops when settled and pauses when hidden (S0340/S1315).
4. **State machine:** hidden, entering, follow, hover(kind), press, drag, reading-dock, exit (S1455).
5. **Reactions per kind** (each different, all the companion's own):
   - **link:** looks at it, and the hat tips;
   - **primary CTA:** leans in, eyes wide, small hop;
   - **phone:** cups an ear;
   - **draggable** (proof box, BA seam): grabs it with both hands; becomes the seam handle;
   - **image or project:** peers, squinting with interest;
   - **map town:** points;
   - **form field:** steps aside (reading-dock) so it never covers input;
   - **text and paragraphs:** docks to the margin;
   - **disabled:** a small head shake.
6. **Click:**
   - an asymmetric 1:2 tap on the tip;
   - on the same frame, the companion's squash and release (S0574);
   - the target's own press is a parallel `scale(.97)`.
7. **Disable:** off on `(hover:none)` / `(pointer:coarse)`, and off under reduced motion (static or none).
8. **Default:** ON (the owner's priority), with a settings toggle kept.
9. **Harness:** `remodeling/src/harness/companion.html`, with every element kind, a frame-by-frame capture and a click test (`cursortest.js`).
