# PLAN-AUDIT-1: raise the plan with the source-level reading

Round 1 of 3. Input: PLAN-v1.md. Output: PLAN.md v2 (PLAN-v1.md kept).
Owner's rule for every round: audits exist to IMPROVE the plan and ADD bolder ideas. Risk, feasibility and mobile may shape HOW an idea is built; they are never a reason to water it down. A round that only removes risk is a failed round.

Lens for this round: **"What do the real sources say that v1 doesn't use yet?"** v1 was designed mostly from the bank's distilled layer. Since then the owner ordered direct reads of the trade-site sources (Stronghold, West London, Green Conception, Hippo, Interlock, Moderna Tak, Oslo Takmester, Zena…), the pack's code folders (MaskedHeading, Specia1ne, Silo, Trionn, CountUp…) and every deep-read slice. This round turns that reading into upgrades.

## (1) Ideas ADDED
1. **The real Springboro light (S1, concept-level).** The hero relight opens at the sun's actual position over Springboro (39.552° N, 84.233° W) at the visitor's current time, from the NOAA solar-position equations (~40 lines, no network): a morning visitor sees low light from the east, an evening visitor golden light from the west, a night visitor the house under its porch light with "We're closed now. Text us and we'll call you after 9." The static LCP plate stays the golden image; only the WebGL relight layer is time-aware. New line under the proof row: *"The light on this page is the light in Springboro right now."* Sources: #341 weather (time of day from local hour), #203 Built Kindly time greeting, Specia1ne hero-time.js (read in full: Intl timezone clock refreshed every 15s). Reduced motion: same light, no sweep.
2. **Storm watch from the National Weather Service (S0 + S2).** On load, one request to the public NWS alerts API (`api.weather.gov/alerts/active?zone=…` for the Warren, Montgomery, Greene and Butler county zones; keyless, public domain, CORS-enabled), cached 10 minutes in sessionStorage. If a Severe Thunderstorm or Tornado Warning is active, a brick banner appears under the header: **"Severe thunderstorm warning for Warren County until 6:45 pm (National Weather Service). Stay off your roof. When it passes: (937) 746-7377."** Fails silent; never shows anything it didn't receive. In S2 V1 the brick line becomes the live warning text when one exists. Source: #341's "on failure show nothing broken" discipline; roofing.md storm-intent finding.
3. **Wind gets under the tabs (S2 V1, beat 3).** The line **"Wind gets under the tabs."** is set as shingle tabs: each letter is a flap hinged at its top edge (transform-origin 50% 0) that lifts in gusts, with a crease shadow underneath, then reseals. Gust strength = scroll velocity (clamped), so a fast scroll is a strong gust. Values: Fold Text #285 (perspective 700, crease shading .55, power3.out .65s, stagger .045) and Animated Opening Type #122 (rest 15°/open 40°, shadow skew per hinge), capped at 38° lift. Reduced motion: letters rest flat, one static lifted "t".
4. **Rain writes the headline (S2 V1, beat 1).** "One night in May." assembles from falling rain in the same 2D canvas as the rain pool: Particle Text #166 "rain" intro (start above the line, fall ease t^1.7, alpha floor .55, deterministic seeds, 96 batched draw groups, 900–2,400 particles mobile/desktop, DPR ≤ 1.25 per #41). The real text is in the DOM (sr-only while the canvas runs; visible text if the canvas fails). Reduced motion: plain text.
5. **Photo of the damage (S5 + estimate engine).** An optional "Add photos of the damage (up to 3)" field (West London's photo-upload intake read in source; Oslo "send us photos of the roof"). Photos are resized on the device (canvas → JPEG 1600px, quality .8, EXIF orientation honoured, EXIF location stripped), uploaded one by one with their own progress; one failed photo never fails the request ("2 of 3 photos sent. Bring the third to the inspection."). No-JS: a plain file input.
6. **Measure your roof (S5, squares only, never a price).** On the paper's "Roof size" line: footprint (sq ft) + roof shape/pitch → "About 26 squares. The real number comes from a tape on your roof." Pitch factors are geometry, not opinion (rise/run 4/12 → ×1.054, 6/12 → ×1.118, 8/12 → ×1.202, 10/12 → ×1.302, 12/12 → ×1.414). Moderna Tak's calculator (read) without its prices, which we may not publish.
7. **"What can change after tear-off" (S5, candour block).** Green Conception's "honest part" (read in source) made Rembrandt's: the only thing that can change after the old roof comes off is soft decking; it's photographed, shown to you, and priced per sheet *in the written estimate before work starts* (exact policy from the owner, §11 Q5). Set as a plain boxed note on the paper.
8. **Real facts for every town (§6 town pages + S11).** Each town page carries three facts nobody can call invented: distance and bearing from the office (computed), the county and its NWS zone (public), and the median year homes were built there (US Census ACS 5-year table B25034, public). Plus any real jobs the owner supplies. That honestly unlocks all 24 pages (Oslo Takmester's district panels, read in source, describe typical building stock per district; this is the data-backed version).
9. **Watchdog boot + first-interaction tags (engineering).** If the motion module hasn't initialised within 4s, `html[data-motion-failed]` is set and the static page stands (West London watchdog, read in source; Specia1ne loader sets the same flag on import failure). Analytics tags load on the first interaction or after 5s idle, whichever comes first (Stronghold, read in source).
10. **The ten-year question (S10).** Hippo's "Will they still be here to stand behind your roof?" (read in source), told with Rembrandt's own fact: *"A lifetime warranty is only as good as the company behind it. We've been here since 1995."* No claims about other firms.
11. **Inspection tools, not just a photo (S3 V1).** "Drop hail": a tap/click on the macro drops a hailstone; its dent is written into the shingle normal map as a Gaussian ring (wave-propagation #138: impacts stored in a 128×1 float DataTexture with age, ring width 3, fade 2s → then permanent dent) and is visible only when the lamp rakes across it. The lesson is felt: you made a dent and you can't see it until the light is low.

## (2) Ideas RAISED to a higher level
1. **S3 V1 becomes literal chiaroscuro.** Outside the lamp the macro desaturates and darkens (Masked Heading SPOT_MASK, read in full: a saturation layer #808080 + a shade layer at .55, both masked by a radial gradient with 9 smootherstep stops; here done in the shader as luminance × mix(0.45, 1, lampMask)). Inside the lamp: full colour and relief. Plus added idea 11.
2. **S1 V3 type takes weight from the light.** Where the lamp passes, the H1's letters gain weight and width (Mona Sans wght 420 → 720, wdth 100 → 118) by Gaussian falloff (Variable Proximity #171: (e^(−2(d/r)²) − e⁻²)/(1 − e⁻²), r = 1.0 × font size, damping 1 − exp(−Δt/80ms), per-letter write cache, loop sleeps when settled); touch gets the idle wave (period 3.2s, peak .7) for 6s then stops. "Light gives weight."
3. **S5 V1 paper is lit by the page's sun.** The paper tooth is an feDiffuseLighting texture (#349 rough paper: fractalNoise .04, 5 octaves, surfaceScale 2) baked at 5 sun azimuths and swapped per chapter (never animated per frame). The stamp, once landed, catches one glare sweep as the sun passes (Glare Hover #372: a 33-stop gradient from the streak profile, core half-width .156, −45°, screen blend, 650ms, once). The request reads as a sentence (Tequila #413 conversational form): "My name is [ ], my house is in [ZIP], and [what's going on]. Call me at [phone], best in the [morning]."
4. **S7 V1 gets a seal.** Beside the signature, a blue-ink circular seal sets in: "LIFETIME WORKMANSHIP · 50-YEAR MATERIALS · SINCE 1995 ·" as a ring of DOM letters (Circular Text #164: real text, aria-label on the ring, spin 40s only while in view, speed spring 60/18, reduced motion: still). The seal is stamped when the signature completes (scale 1.2 → 1, 260ms overshoot) and the noon light glints across it once (#372).
5. **S0 V1 header knows the office state.** A chip next to the phone: "Open now, until 5" with a 6px dot and the Shazam double-ring pulse (#255: 1.2s ease-out, scale 3 → 1, second ring +.6s), or "Closed. Text us" after hours. Plus the NWS banner slot (added idea 2).
6. **S8 V2 "On the table" becomes physical.** Tapping a print pushes the others away (Push Grid #157, read: Flip from captured rects, clicked print reparented to fullscreen, others classified N/S/E/W and pushed to the edge rows, 0.9s expo; demo-2 random ±50° rotation reduced to ±12°). Close returns everything.
7. **S2 V2 "The year in one roof" gains Ohio's four threats as tabs** (Interlock "Every climate" tablist, read in source): Hail · Wind · Freeze-thaw · Ice dams, each with what it does to a roof and what to check, and the current month auto-selected. Proper ARIA tabs (Interlock's own roving tabindex code read).
8. **S13 V1 answers are signed.** Each answer ends "Answered by Rachel, office" (owner-approved wording; Solterra #507 names a person in FAQ answers).
9. **S9 V1 numeral gets its substantiation line** directly under it (Tequila #413 "every stat carries a one-line substantiation"): "295 Google reviews, average 4.9 stars, checked on [date of build]."
