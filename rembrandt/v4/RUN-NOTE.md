## The concept in 5 lines
1. Rembrandt is remembered for light that reveals; Rembrandt Roofing's real job is finding storm damage that hides until the light is low.
2. The homepage is one day of light: the scroll position is the sun, and chapter labels are clock times (golden hour → storm night → dawn inspection → morning estimate → noon signature → evening).
3. The hero is lit by the sun's real position over Springboro right now, and a live National Weather Service warning for the four counties appears as a banner.
4. You sweep an inspector's lamp over hail-bruised shingles and the bruises appear; the roof's layers fly into a written estimate that is the form itself, stamped in blue ink when sent.
5. Every claim links to the body that issues it; nothing is invented; the phone, (937) 746-7377, is never more than one tap away.
