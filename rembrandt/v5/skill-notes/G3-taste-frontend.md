## S0095 design-taste-frontend (tasteskill v2)

### Premise and warning
- A generated page = "centred headline over three lines, a little label above it, a row of icon cards, a soft gradient, a few thin rules and a monospace caption". Replace defaults with decisions: (1) choose a direction among three dealt, (2) the idea comes from the subject, (3) a house style with no ornaments, measured, (4) look at what you made (screenshots at three widths, juror rubric, fix, look again).
- **Removing ornaments is only half the job.** "Blind jurors rightly prefer a richer page with tells over an empty page without them." The other half is substance: type set larger, whole fields of colour, real pictures of this subject's world, every section carrying its full content. "Do not trade it for restraint."
- Page (read and scrolled) vs app screen (worked in). A homepage is a **page**; app-screens.md does not apply.

### The run (in order; steps 1–5 decide whether the page is any good)
1. **Read the brief.** Find what is real: subject, audience, the one thing the page must get someone to do, stack, brand (colours, fonts, logo), and **what imagery truly exists**. If vague, decide and state your reading in one line; ask only when a missing fact blocks the work. **Inventory 8–12 things that belong to this client and no competitor**: its name and what the name means; address, street, neighbourhood and what it looks like; the numbers (year, price…); its constraint; materials and colours; rituals and hours; printed things of its world. Winning pages in blind tests were built from an item on this list. Each item is also a picture search query.
2. **Write the argument.** List the page as claims with proof, one line per section. Delete any section without a claim. Avoids the stock "hero, logos, three features, testimonial, pricing, call to action".
3. **Draw the direction** (draw.mjs deals 3 of 20 directions; choose the one that carries something only this client has, not the most familiar; write one line why). Adopt the sheet; don't blend with your habitual layout. Redraw a slot only for a stated reason (brand constraint, missing asset, section can't hold real content); never "to get something more comfortable". **Check the palette against the subject**: if the subject has unmistakable colours of its own, keep the dealt proportions (one ground, one ink, one accent, fields) and take the colours from the subject.
4. **Find the idea.** Fill both blanks from the subject before building: "On this page, **[something that belongs to the subject]** becomes **[the device the direction calls for]**." Look at: its output, its object/shape, its name, its place, its ritual, its artefact (ticket, label, receipt, manual), its opposite. **Competitor test:** put the nearest real competitor in the sentence; if it still works, the idea belongs to the category. "Clean and modern" is not an idea.
   - **One live thing:** one thing the visitor can do, or that differs each visit, made from the idea. "In blind tests every winning page had one and the jurors named it first." Rules: uses the subject's real content; works on a phone with a thumb; plain fallback without script; never blocks reading; one thing, not a playground; built in step 6 as part of the page, not added as motion.
   - Scroll scenes carry the idea too: decide what each shows from the inventory; "A scene filled with pictures that could belong to anyone is a screensaver."
5. **Write the words** as a plain-text copy deck before markup. Every headline **two lines or fewer at every width**; "about 32 characters is safe everywhere"; gallery heroes median **5 words, 32 characters**. One supporting sentence directly under the headline (**32–48px below it**), shown with it, never docked at the foot or held back by animation. One primary action, named for what it does, as a real button on the first screen. First screen answers the visitor's first question in large type. Every section the visitor came for has its own heading and its own nav place. Write the full content; "Thin sections are the commonest reason a clean page loses to a busier one." Sample details allowed where brief has gaps (list in report); **proof is never invented**: no customers, quotes, reviews, press, logos or business statistics.
6. **Build it, still** (motion off in your head; "a page that needs animation to look finished is not finished"). Order: (1) tokens as CSS custom properties, everything references tokens; (2) the hero at desktop and phone, "big thing big and the small things small"; (3) each section from its blueprint; **neighbouring sections must not share a layout**; carry the idea into every section incl. pricing, FAQ, footer; (4) imagery, preference order: project's own → generated → real pictures of the subject's world from open collections → drawn in code from the subject → dealt generic photos → type and colour alone. When the project has a real picture of its own process/product, the **first screen shows it large (about 40% of the screen or more)**. Never write a sentence about where pictures come from. **Floor: at least four real pictures, usually six to twelve**; SVG drawings don't count. Add one moving picture where an honest clip exists (muted loop with poster). (5) the live thing; (6) scroll scenes; (7) **fullness**: each desktop screen a poster, one dominant thing; one deliberate rest per page max; two or more near-empty screens fails. Running text in full ink at **16px or more**; grey only for secondary notes. (8) **the frame**: one pair of equal outer margins at every width; if text is in the left half the right half holds its picture/second column/list/figure, never nothing; no stretch taller than about half a screen holds nothing; sections **120–240px apart on desktop**; every section opens with a heading naming it; first screen carries the title in an `h1`. (9) states and semantics: real links/buttons, visible focus, alt text, phone layout its own composition; every control incl. the live thing works by keyboard in reading order with an accessible name (visible label, or `aria-label` if icon only).
7. **Gate.** lint (banned patterns, stock phrases) and inspect at **1440, 768, 390**: headline line counts, type sizes, fonts actually loaded, colours, rules, shadows, gradients, contrast, overflow, equal margins, huddled sections, blank stretches, title, untitled sections, text against the screen edge. Fix every FAIL; treat each WARN as wrong unless defensible "to a designer in one sentence". Inspect what ships (production build), and open the file from the file system once.
8. **Look.** Open every screenshot; critique with critique.md (14 passes, weighted score, caps); fix the top three; re-inspect; **three rounds at most**. "If a problem is visible in a screenshot, it is real, whatever the code says."
9. **Move.** Required, more than one fade: inspector fails a page with **under 8** elements moving during load+scroll, warns **under 16**. Four parts around any scenes: (1) the signature move, one, done well; (2) an entrance for the parts of every section: heading, then text, then picture, **staggered 60–90ms, rising 16–32px with opacity, once**, not one fade for the whole block, never content hidden if script fails; (3) feedback on every control (press, hover, focus); (4) one moving picture (muted loop, slow scale/drift on a photo, or scroll-linked hero move). Transform and opacity only; every move has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback that keeps content and drops travel. Moves that change colour/layout of whole screens (colour by chapter, pinned story) are composition: build in step 6.
10. **Report** only when: 0 FAIL at 1440/768/390 on the final page; every screenshot opened after the last change; scenes checked (desktop, phone, reduced motion); "A stranger reading the first screen for five seconds can say what this is and what to do"; every section has a heading, margins match, nothing empty; last critique written in §4 shape. Report: direction and redraws, idea sentence and live thing, final scores and shortfalls, every found picture with credit, stand-ins, sample content to swap. "Do not claim a check you did not run."

### House rules (every build; banned → instead)
- **Headlines ≤2 lines at every width** (every h1–h6 and the first screen's main line) → cut words, then widen measure, size last.
- **Clear title on first screen** in the one `h1`; heading on every section.
- **Equal outer margins; no section huddled on one side** → close every void with something on its far side.
- **No blank stretches** taller than about half a screen; a deliberate rest (placed picture or colour field) once per page.
- **Statements are paragraphs**: `<p>`/`<blockquote>`, **36px or less on desktop, 24px on a phone**, about **25 words, three lines**. "Jurors read a 42px sentence over four lines as a four-line headline."
- **No eyebrows** (small label above a heading, pill badge above headline, "New ·" chip).
- **No monospace** outside real code.
- **No index numbering** (01/02/03, (001), "Step 2") → position, size, words; a real `<ol>` if order matters.
- **No thin rules** (hairlines, 1px outlined boxes, grid lines, crosshairs, crop marks) → space, tone step, change of scale, filled shape.
- **No gradients** (backgrounds, buttons, text) → flat colour or real photo; **a neutral scrim under text on a photo is the one exception**.
- **No shadows on things at rest; no glows** → tone step; shadows only on what truly floats (menu, dialog).
- **No assistant palette**: no yellow-cream ground with terracotta/rust; no violet/indigo.
- **No tiny uppercase letter-spaced labels** → sentence case, readable size.
- **No icon-topped feature cards, no rows of identical cards.**
- **No glass** (backdrop blur panels, floating pill navigation) → plain bar: wordmark, a few links, one action.
- **No emoji, gradient blobs, decorative shapes, fake dashboards of grey bars.**
- **No styled accent word** in a headline (italic, serif, coloured, underlined).
- **Two typefaces at most, usually one.**
- Exempt: real `<pre>/<code>`, real tables and form fields (may have lines), `data-ui` interface fragments.
- Study basis (score ≥7.5 vs <6.5): icon feature cards 1% vs 28%, pill badges 14% vs 34%, gradients 22% vs 52%, shadows 24% vs 48%, tiny tracked caps 16% vs 57%. Mono labels, index numbers, hairline grids are *more* common on award sites; banned anyway as "costume".
- "Removing ornament leaves space. Never fill it with another ornament." Fill with scale, full content, a picture made for the subject, or flat colour.

### Laws of the craft
- **Type:** two or three sizes do the visible work; median headline:body ratio **4.3**; largest type median **8.4vw** on top sites vs **5.0vw** weak. Display tracking **-0.02 to -0.05em**, leading **0.85–1.05**. Text **16–20px**, leading **1.45–1.6**, **45–75 characters** per line. **Five sizes ceiling.** Left-aligned by default; centre at most one moment.
- **Space:** one grid, one pair of tight margins (**16–32px**); large things run to the edges; text blocks share **two or three left edges** on the whole page. Asymmetry inside the grid, never in the frame. Section gap **120–240px** desktop (study median about 240). Each section differs from its neighbours; none is a card grid.
- **Colour:** one ground, one ink, one accent; accent on the action and perhaps one other thing; running text in full ink; surfaces separated by tone step, never line or shadow; a section may change the whole field colour; "Nothing fades into anything."
- **Imagery:** one dominant image beats six small; top sites' hero media covers at least half the first screen **68%** of the time. Same crop ratios, same treatment, **square corners** (73% of sites). Nothing ever lies on top of a headline or statement.
- **Components:** buttons flat, one fill, **square-cornered or fully round, decided once** (top sites 52% square, 23% pill; "the 9–16px 'soft' radius is the weak-site default"). Most strong heroes have no button or one. Nav: wordmark, **three to five** plain links, one action, no bar chrome. A control explains itself by form, label and place; field limits stay visible, tied with `aria-describedby`. Footer is a designed close, not a sitemap; on most strong sites the name returns at full width.

### Special cases
- **Existing brand:** keep palette/type, map onto roles; if the brand requires a banned device (e.g. its accent), keep it and say so.
- **Redesign:** (1) record the before (screenshots, measurements); (3) keep the stack, content and what works; no framework migration; (4) order of work, biggest effect for least risk: **type → colour → the frame → states → stock components replaced**; (5) prove it: before/after at every width, click every link/form/menu that worked before.
- **No form backend:** the primary button goes to the real destination; otherwise it opens an email (`mailto:` with subject and body, "with the visitor's choices filled in"). "Never fake a payment form."

### colour.md
- Study: 41% dark, 59% light; buttons neutral on 52%; top sites use a median of **two section grounds** vs one.
- Roles: `--bg, --surface (one tone step), --ink, --muted (passes contrast on --bg), --accent, --accent-ink`, plus `--field-n` with own inks. No raw hex in components; no extra greys between roles.
- **Proportion:** ground **70–90%**; ink = text and perhaps one inverted section; **accent under 5% of any screen**, on the action and at most one other recurring thing. Accent is not for headings, icons, bullets, borders, underlines. "A headline is ink."
- Surfaces: `--surface` **2–6 lightness points** from `--bg`; inputs filled, `border: 0`; `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--accent); outline-offset: 2px; }`. No panel in panel. Images need no frame. A **thick bar (4px or more)** is allowed as a graphic; a 1px line is not.
- **Colour fields:** meet edge to edge with a hard line; each field has its own ink (never `--muted` on a field; field ink at full or 70% if it passes); neighbours differ in **lightness**, not only hue; order like chapters: "open strong, rest, surprise, close dark or very light"; meta theme colour and nav follow the first field.
- Dark pages: `color-scheme: dark`; deep tinted ground, soft light ink, not #fff on #000; check `--muted` at 4.5:1; depth from lighter surface, never glows; lighter font weights.
- Photos: when photos carry colour, interface stays neutral, ink from the darkest tone in the imagery. Never wash a photo with brand colour. Text over a photo needs a calm region; **neutral scrim black or ink at 30–55% opacity**, over the text area only. A flat field sampled from a photo (its sky, its shadow) ties a section to it.
- Contrast: body **4.5:1**; display and large numerals **3:1**; dealt palettes **7:1** ink on ground; inspector fails under **2.6:1**; hover/focus raise contrast.
- Banned: cream (hue roughly **22–62**, light, saturated) + rust/clay/burnt-orange; violet/indigo. Warmth = low-chroma stone/bone/putty (**saturation under about 15%**), deep green/brown/oxblood ink, photographs, or a full warm field used flat. "Warmth is not beige."

### copy.md
- Argument first, one line each: who is reading; what is this (as its maker would tell a friend); the single thing to believe after one screen; what makes them believe it; what they do next. Then claims-with-proof per section; "the order of the argument is the order of the page."
- **Two-line arithmetic:** `characters per line ≈ line width px ÷ (font size px × k)`, **k ≈ 0.40** condensed, **0.50** most grotesks/serifs at display size, **0.58** wide/extended/all-caps. Phone 390 (350px line): 44px → ~16/line → **~32 chars**; 34px → ~21 → ~42. Tablet 768 (690px) 64px → ~44. Desktop 1100px at 104px → ~42. Half width 620px at 72px → ~34. So **about 32 characters (four to six words)** survives everywhere; up to ~42 if the phone size drops to the mid-30s.
- Control the break: `max-width` in `ch`, `text-wrap: balance`, author `<br>` and hide under a breakpoint if it wraps; never fix length only by shrinking ("A timid 28px headline of nine words is worse than five words at 64px"); no orphan on line two. Section headings **two to five words**.
- Statement size: **36px desktop, 32px tablet, 24px phone**, ~25 words, ~three lines (four on phone).
- Shapes that work: the name; what it is; the claim (one sentence a competitor couldn't sign); the instruction (verb first); the place or the moment ("Lisbon, since 1984"). Avoid: the mad-lib, the tricolon, the correction ("not just…", "More than a…"), "reimagined/redefined/made simple", "Welcome to…", "Meet…", unasked questions, headline needing a colon, one styled word. Sentence case; no full stop unless voice uses them everywhere.
- Supporting line: one sentence, **at most about 22 words**, concrete; never repeats the headline. Body: specific noun, real number. Buttons: **one to three words**, primary keeps one name everywhere; **one primary per screen**, second action a plain text link; at most one arrow, on the primary. Nav: wordmark, up to five links, single nouns, one action.
- Proof: never invent customers, quotes, logos, user counts, **ratings**, benchmarks. Use facts from the brief (price, place, year, materials).
- Generated tells: unlock, unleash, supercharge, elevate, empower, streamline, seamless, effortless, robust, cutting-edge, next-generation, world-class, all-in-one, blazing fast; "Field notes", "Our philosophy", "Quietly trusted by"; furniture text ("Est. 2024", coordinates, "Scroll", "(Scroll to explore)", status dots, middle-dot strings); em dash habit ("One or two on a page read as writing; ten read as a model"); captions narrating the design; "Step 1"; three parallel items everywhere ("Use the number the content has").
- Micro-typography: curly quotes, real ellipsis, en dash ranges; `&nbsp;` number–unit and before an orphaning last word; `tabular-nums` in columns; `text-wrap: balance` headings, `pretty` paragraphs; no all-caps over three words (if caps, **0.02–0.06em** tracking); **no text under 13px**; no centred paragraphs over three lines; no justified text.

### critique.md
- Judge screenshots, never code. Order: desktop fold → desktop sheets → mobile fold → mobile sheets.
- **14 passes:** (1) one second, then five (one answer; stranger can say what/who/what next); (2) squint (two or three masses); (3) cover the logo (competitor's? does the idea reach pricing/FAQ/footer?); (4) edges (count left x-positions: two or three; six = placed one at a time); (5) space and frame (section gap > internal gaps; holes vs rests; equal margins; half-empty sections; blank > half screen); (6) type (jump ≥3×; >5 sizes; orphans; >75 chars; centred >3 lines); (7) colour proportions; (8) images (≥4 real, a moving one, shared ratios, one light/grade); (9) sameness (one word per section layout; same word twice in a row or three times = template); (10) phone is its own composition; (11) fullness (each screen a poster; statement held three screens?); (12) substance; (13) collisions (nothing over headline/statement/button; nothing off the phone edge; sideways-scrolling rows read as cut off unless plainly deliberate); (14) the competitor and the live thing.
- **Score:** design **40%**, usability **30%**, creativity **20%**, content **10%**; `overall = 0.4·design + 0.3·usability + 0.2·creativity + 0.1·content`. Real Sites of the Day ≈ **7.5–8.0**; "An honest 8.5 is rare"; if you score yourself 9, rescore.
- **Caps:** any FAIL → overall ≤**6.0**; 3+ line headline, eyebrow, mono labels, 01/02/03, decorative gradient, resting shadow, decorative hairline → overall ≤**6.5**; page could belong to anyone → creativity ≤**6**; idea passes for competitor → creativity ≤**7**; fewer than four real pictures → design ≤**6.5**; barely moves or one identical fade everywhere → design ≤**7**; no live thing → creativity ≤**7.5**; placeholder → content ≤**5**; two+ near-empty desktop screens or small grey running text → design ≤**6.5**; requested section missing/thin → content ≤**6**; no real primary button on first screen → usability ≤**7**; five-second pass fails → usability ≤**6**; unequal margins/huddled/blank > half screen → design ≤**6.5**; untitled text section → usability ≤**7**; anything covering a headline → usability ≤**6**; blank band where a reveal didn't fire or clipped content → usability ≤**5**.
- Format: `ROUND n / Seen in one second / Idea / Live thing / Layout words by section / Scores … → overall / Caps applied / Top three problems (screenshot + fix)`. Name problems like a designer ("the testimonial section is the third split layout in a row").
- **Fix order:** idea → hierarchy → composition → typography → imagery → colour → detail → **motion last**. Stop after three rounds; if round three < **7.5**, say so plainly.

### imagery.md
- Study: one piece of media covers ≥half the first screen on 60% (top 68%); only 15% of heroes have no media; top pages median **37 images vs 5** weak. Oct 2026: median page **7 pictures** (top 8, lowest 2); footage on **42%**; **94%** square corners; two ratios hold three-quarters of pictures.
- Generated images: brief like a photographer (subject, angle, light, background, crop, "no text"); hero first, rest match its light. Found pictures: commercial licences only (CC0, PD, CC BY, CC BY-SA; no NC/ND), ≥1000px, twice display width for full-bleed, one family (all one era/kind), no watermark, no recognisable living person who could pass as staff/customers; **never presented as the client's own**; download, don't hotlink; credit in the footer.
- **Cropping:** a small set of ratios (e.g. **4:5** portraits, **16:10** landscapes, **1:1** details); go large; `object-fit: cover` with per-image `object-position`; let images touch edges; overlap once per screen max (**40–80px**); square corners (or one radius for all); **no frames, borders, shadows, device mock-ups**; captions one short line, small, soft ink, left-aligned; most images need none.
- **Treatment, one for the page:** untouched (default); greyscale `filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.05)` for every image or none; multiply on a field; darkened `brightness(0.85) contrast(1.05)`. Not treatments: colour washes, duotone, blur, grain, vignettes, tilted polaroids. Text scrim: **solid block 40–55% black, or a fade from 55% black to transparent over the lower third**; never coloured.
- Drawings (when no picture exists): the subject's own things, one graphic language, palette colours, **three levels of detail**, flat tone steps not gradients, drawn large; not 3D blobs, isometric clip art, people at laptops.
- Markup: always `width`/`height`; `loading="lazy"` except the hero (`fetchpriority="high"`); alt says what the picture shows; container `background-color` in ground/surface tone so layout is whole before load.
- **Video:** clips **5–20s** looping without jump; under **5 MB section / 10 MB hero**; 720p plenty behind type; clean (no slate/logo); found clips one per page (two max), own footage can carry more; **one moving picture per screen**. Markup: `autoplay muted loop playsinline preload="metadata" poster=… width height aria-hidden="true"`; poster required (only 31% of studied clips had one; blank boxes recorded as faults). Play only in view (IntersectionObserver threshold **0.2**), none under reduced motion (poster shows). On a clip: one headline, one line, one action max; check contrast across the whole loop (**4.5:1, 3:1 for 24px+**). Nothing important plays on hover only.
- **Where pictures go (Oct 2026, 91 sites):** pictures come "all or nothing": **42%** of screens none, **19%** ≥90% picture; average screen **36%** picture (top **30%**, leaving **28%** of screens pictureless). Text is **8%** of the median screen. Off-centre beats centred (939 vs 273). Phone carries more picture (first phone screen 27% vs 17% desktop). **Three to five placements** per page (≤2 average 7.31; ≥3 ~7.5).
- Placements (mean score with/without): split **7.50**; held picture while text scrolls **7.52**; inset frame that grows **7.52**; cut-out object on flat field **7.57** (highest); one small picture beside a statement **7.56**; small pictures between words **7.52**; uneven mosaic **7.49**; full-bleed band **7.48**; even grid 7.48; horizontal gallery 7.44 ("lifts nothing"); **3D gallery 7.30 — do not build one**.
- Geometry highlights: **Split** picture **42%** of width median (33–49%, up to two-thirds), full section height (680×850, 720×900), touching the viewport on its outer side; text on the 50% line, top-aligned or docked low; phone: picture first, full width, 4:5. **Full-bleed band** 100% wide, **100–150vh**, one every **three or four screens**, parallax oversize **4–10%**, text docked **32–64px** from a corner; phone portrait re-crop, text in bottom quarter. **Held picture** 100vh sticky inside 2–6 screens, text rows ~150px apart; phone releases the stick. **Inset frame grows** once per page, from **35–50%** width (or a 16px inset) to full over **720–2,700px** scroll, radius to 0, nothing else moves. **Mosaic** 5–12 pictures, two or three widths (32/48/65%), vertical offsets **120–190px**, one or two ratios, caption under each in text face, no numbers. **Horizontal gallery** cards **25–35%** width, 16–20px gaps, three plus a cut fourth; phone one card at **75–80%**; marquee **60–120s** per loop. **Panel over held picture**: solid, square-cornered, no rounded sheet tops, no glass. **Cut-out**: object **35–60%** width on a ground from its own colours; phone 80–90%. **Picture in display type**: type stays in front; phone type ~**15vw**. **Docked corner card** ~216×144–240×140, 30–43px from corner, one per page.
- Rules for a generator: (1) **30–40%** of area is picture, ≥1 screen in 4 none; (2) a leading picture takes **≥60%** of its screen, an accent **<10%**; (3) 3–5 placements, no neighbours sharing one; (4) **6–12 pictures, never fewer than four**; (5) two ratios for **≥75%** (defaults 4:5, 16:9 or 3:2); (6) square corners (or one **4px** radius); (7) splits 42–50% or two-thirds; (8) bands 100–150vh, oversize ≤10%, text ≥32px in; (9) one inset frame per page; (10) galleries three and a cut fourth; (11) one moving picture per screen, clips **5–15s**, hero ≤10MB 1920×1080, inline ≤5MB 1280×720; (12) only display text on a picture; body 16px+ beside or under.

### layout.md
- Strong pages: tight margins (≤32px on ~45 of 114; centred 1100–1300px container is "the usual frame of the ones the panel called stock"); text hangs from an off-centre line (a third, 42%, midpoint); two or three type sizes; one thing per screen; sections told apart by ground; sections recomposed; **system broken once on purpose**; often no nav bar; endings are designed scenes; **few words: median 575, some of the best under 200**.
- Grid: `--margin: clamp(1rem, 2.2vw, 2rem)` (16–32px); `--gutter: clamp(0.75rem, 1.4vw, 1.5rem)`; 12 columns; no reflexive `max-width:1200px` page wrapper (constrain only the text measure in `ch`); cap at **1760–1920px** on very wide screens. Never draw the grid.
- Text: reuse **two or three anchor lines**; off-centre beats centred; opposite ends of one baseline; centred = one axis, one element, wide flanks; long copy rows = heading left third, body right two-thirds.
- **Empty space:** a void is placed only if something closes it (the picture, a picture across the void, type spanning it). Holes come from: over-stretched section gaps (bands of 250–760px), text in one half only, **content arriving late (reveal not fired)**, short block stretched to a screen, pinned scene holding nothing, uneven picture heights. Sections **120–240px** apart desktop, **72–112px** phone; >~40% of a screen of plain ground between sections is a hole; heading has ≥2× space above vs below; **content in place on first paint**; `min-height: 100vh` only for sections that fill the screen.
- **Frame:** equal margins (match to a few px), **never under 16px**, nav on the same margins; bleeds start from the margin; inspector warns when one empty block takes **28%** of the first screen. `h1` at display size (**≥32px desktop**, clear first screens mostly **50–150px**), two lines max, says what this is; if the h1 is the name, the line under it at **18px+** says what the thing is. Section headings: 82% of text sections have one, median **57px** (40–96px), ~3.5× body.
- Sections: idea survives to the last pixel; no two neighbours share a layout; alternate dense/empty, image/type, light/dark; repeated modules only for archives; break the system once; short pages are fine.
- Nav: wordmark, 3–5 text links, one action, no bar/border/background (median 3 links); fixed nav must never sit on content; phone: wordmark + one action, links in a full-screen sheet behind a text "Menu"; nav ≤ about **a tenth of the first screen**, one row on phone ("a nav that ate the hero cost a whole point of usability").
- Endings: name at full width, hero inverted, one contact fact as a headline, or one quiet line; link-column footers only appear as criticism. Practical facts (address, hours, legal) in text face at readable size.
- Phone: its own composition; one dominant thing; re-author breaks, re-crop portrait; margins **16–20px**; body **16–18px**; touch targets **≥44px** high.
- Failures (<6.8): category template (~20/54), centred headline + pill + two buttons + screenshot, distinctive hero then stock modules, default typeface, small/low-contrast text, never saying what it is, thin content over many screens, **gates, preloaders, entry pop-ups**, shrunk phone layout, fixed elements over content.

### motion.md

3. **One live thing that converts.** For example "What's your roof doing?": large thumb-sized choices (missing shingles, a leak stain, hail or wind damage, just old) that fill a real `mailto:`/`sms:` estimate request with subject and body, next to `tel:+19377467377`. Plain links without script, fully keyboard-operable, never blocks reading. A before/after drag on a real job pair is the other honest option. Pick one for the page, not both.
4. **Words budget (the owner's main complaint).** Aim for under ~200–575 words on the whole homepage. Hero h1 ≤32 characters, two lines at 1440/768/390 (e.g. name-led h1 "Rembrandt Roofing" plus an 18px+ line saying "Family roofers in Springboro, Ohio, since 1995."). Section headings 2–5 words; statements ≤25 words at ≤36px desktop / 24px phone; held-scene statements 4–12 words; captions 1–4 words. No em-dash habit, no "elevate/seamless", no tricolons, no eyebrows.
5. **Proof is real and set big.** 1995, 4.9, 295 and A+ as rolling figures at display size (3–4 figures, 1100ms expo, real numbers kept for screen readers); link "295 Google reviews" to the real Google listing. No invented quotes. Quote real reviews only if we have them verbatim. No decorative star rows. Badges (BBB, Owens Corning) only as real marks we're allowed to use, small and in one row, never as icon cards.
6. **Conversion on every screen.** One primary button named once everywhere (e.g. "Free estimate", 1–3 words), as a real button on the first screen; the phone number as a plain text link beside it. One primary per screen, at most one arrow. Accent colour (<5% of any screen) goes only on that action and maybe one other recurring thing. The phone nav is wordmark + the call/estimate action + a "Menu" text button; the nav takes at most a tenth of the first screen.
7. **Image-led within the measured limits.** Hero media ≥50% of the first screen (the firm's own best job photo, full-bleed or a 42–50% split touching the viewport edge). 6–12 real photos on the page, never fewer than four. About 30–40% of scrolled area is picture, at least 1 screen in 4 with none. Two ratios hold ≥75% (4:5 and 16:9 or 3:2). Square corners everywhere. One treatment (untouched, or all greyscale, which could carry the Rembrandt light idea). No frames, shadows, duotone or grain.
8. **Text on photos.** Only display text, one line and one action. Use a flat neutral scrim (30–55% black, or a 55%→0 fade over the lower third) and check 4.5:1 (3:1 at 24px+) at the text's own position. Body copy goes beside or under the photos, at 16px+ in full ink.
9. **1|2|3 versions must truly differ in placement, not only styling.** Give each version of a section a different placement family from the ranked table: split, held picture with passing captions, inset frame that grows, cut-out object on a flat field, uneven mosaic, full-bleed band, small pictures between words, picture inside display type. No 3D gallery. Plan the families so neighbouring sections never share one in the default 1-1-1 combination, and ideally in any combination. Every version must pass all house rules on its own.
10. **The picker itself is a control, not an ornament.** Plain "1 2 3" (never "01/02/03"). Use `role="radiogroup"` or buttons with `aria-pressed`, with accessible names ("Hero version 2 of 3"), keyboard operable, ≥44px targets, square or round matching the buttons. Swapping a version is a quick UI move (~240ms opacity, `--ease-out`; instant under reduced motion). It must kill and rebuild that section's ScrollTriggers and call `ScrollTrigger.refresh()`. It should never cover a headline.
11. **Motion budget.** Two or three GSAP ScrollTrigger scenes per page (≤4 in any version combination), each held 1–4 screens with a quiet section between them, scrubbed linear and locked to the scrollbar. One signature move, likely an opening window or shutter on the hero roof photo. Per-section entrances: heading → text → picture, 60–90ms stagger, 12–32px rise, once at 85–90% viewport, varied by content. Press/hover feedback on every control (press 140ms scale 0.97, hover colour 180ms). One moving picture per screen (Ken Burns at `--ease-soft`, 106–110% oversize, <8% travel). At least 8 moving elements, aiming for 16+. Use the tokens verbatim: expo `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`, out `(0.22,1,0.36,1)`, in-out `(0.76,0,0.24,1)`.
12. **"Fun" without the gimmicks the skill bans.** Fun comes from scale, colour-by-chapter hard cuts (header ink follows in 300ms), a pushed or scaling wordmark, a sideways gallery of real jobs (<3 screens, 1:1, keyboard), and the live thing. Not allowed: custom cursors, scroll hijack, scramble or typewriter text, bounce or overshoot, preloaders or counters, pulsing dots, hover-zoom on every card.
13. **First frame and speed.** No preloader, even though the file is large. Paint the h1, the supporting line, the hero photo and the button on the first frame. Keep the load sequence to ≤4 elements, settled by 1.2s. Hero `<img>` gets `fetchpriority="high"`, explicit `width`/`height`, and a ground colour sampled from it. Other images get `loading="lazy" decoding="async"` and explicit dimensions.
14. **Single-file constraints.** Inline minified GSAP + ScrollTrigger (skill pins `gsap@3.15.0`; Lenis `1.3.26`, optional at lerp 0.1–0.14, off for touch and reduced motion), and say so in the file header. Inline fonts as subset woff2 (one family, at most two; two or three weights) with a same-kind fallback stack. Inline images as compressed WebP/AVIF at about 2× CSS width, capped at ~2400px. Data-URI video is heavy (limits are ≤5MB inline and ≤10MB hero), so prefer a photo drift over video.
15. **Colour.** Map the firm's real logo colours onto ground/ink/accent. If there are none, take colours from the subject: shingle charcoal, Ohio sky and roof-deck timber, held to the proportions. Avoid cream ground + rust/terracotta (a classic roofing palette and a banned assistant palette) and violet. If the brand truly owns a rust or red, keep it and note the exception. Fields meet with hard edges and differ in lightness. Each field gets its own ink. No gradients except the photo scrim. No resting shadows, hairlines, or `#000`/`#fff` pairs on dark chapters.
16. **Type.** One distinctive family (not Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans or Helvetica). Five sizes at most, using the step tokens. Display ≥4× body, aiming at ~8vw+ for the largest type. Display tracking -0.02 to -0.05em, leading 0.85–1.05. Body 17–19px at leading 1.5, max 62ch, left-aligned, centring one moment at most. Use tabular figures for the stats. Nothing under 13px, and no tiny tracked caps.
17. **Frame checks to run by hand** (the inspector isn't available). Equal 16–32px margins at 1440/768/390, with the nav on the same margins. Sections 120–240px apart on desktop, 72–112px on phone. No half-empty section, no blank band taller than half a screen, at most one deliberate rest. One h1, a heading on every section. Two or three left edges for the whole page. No sideways scroll at 390.
18. **Reduced motion and no-JS.** Gate hidden states behind `html.js` and `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. The reduced page is the finished composition: scenes at rest, figures at their values, photos still, loops stopped, picker swaps instant. Keep opacity/colour changes and drop travel, scale and parallax. Never leave a blank band if a reveal fails.
19. **Critique loop.** Before handing over, score each round on the Awwwards weighting (0.4/0.3/0.2/0.1) with caps. Examples: no live thing caps creativity at 7.5; one identical fade everywhere caps design at 7; no first-screen button caps usability at 7. Fix in order: idea → hierarchy → composition → type → imagery → colour → detail → motion. Run at most three rounds, and report honestly if under 7.5.
20. **Redesign hygiene.** Screenshot the rejected previous version as the "before". Keep what worked (real content, phone, links). Check the new page against the owner's four complaints (too much text, document-like, not fun, poor animation) and the before/after word count, line counts and moving-element count.
