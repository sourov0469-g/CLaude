Storm damage and insurance

What to look for after a storm.

Tap a mark on the roof. Your insurance company decides the claim; we give your adjuster the photos.

Torn or missing shingles.

Wind lifts tabs and tears them away, and the layer beneath shows. We photograph each one.

Dents on vents and flashing.

Hail leaves small dents on metal. They show up in close photos.

Grit in the gutter.

Shingle granules washing out of the downspout after a storm are worth a look.

A fallen limb.

The roof under it needs a close look.
