Storm damage, and the insurance claim.

Bunk Walker built this firm restoring storm-damaged homes. Here is who does what.

What we do

  1. Inspect the roof and photograph the damage.
  2. Write the repair up as a line-item scope.
  3. Meet your adjuster at the roof, if you want us there.
  4. Start only the work you approve in writing.

What your insurance company decides

  • Whether the claim is approved.
  • What your policy pays.

We never promise how a claim will turn out. That decision is your insurer's.
