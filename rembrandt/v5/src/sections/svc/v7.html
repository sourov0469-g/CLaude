The Ohio year, job by job.

Ohio weather sets the work. Choose a mark on the year to see which job it brings.

  • Hail season, April to July: storm and hail damage, and the insurance claim.
  • Freeze and thaw, November to March: roof repair.
  • Ice dams, January and February: loft ventilation.
  • Any month: roof replacement, siding and gutters.

Also: commercial roofing, EPDM and TPO, roof coatings, gutter cleaning, blown-in insulation, brick veneer, skylights and loft ventilation.
