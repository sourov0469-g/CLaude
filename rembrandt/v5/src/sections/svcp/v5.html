The main six

  1. Roof replacementTorn off to the deck, and the deck checked.

    We tear the old roof off down to the deck and check the deck before anything new goes on. Rembrandt is an Owens Corning Platinum Preferred contractor. You get a written, line-by-line estimate first, and no work starts without your written approval.

  2. Roof repairMost repairs finish in one visit.

    Our repair crew carries flashing, pipe boots and matching shingles on the truck. That is why most repairs finish in one visit. You still get the repair in writing before we start.

  3. Storm and hail damagePhotographed for your adjuster.

    Bunk Walker built this firm restoring storm-damaged homes. Ohio averages 19 hail days a year, and hail season runs April to July. We inspect the roof and photograph the damage for your insurance adjuster.

  4. Insurance claimsWe document. Your insurer decides.

    We write the repair up as a line-item scope for your claim. We can meet your adjuster at the roof, if you want us there. Your insurance company decides the claim and what your policy pays. We never promise how a claim will turn out.

  5. SidingJames Hardie and vinyl.

    We install James Hardie and vinyl siding. It goes on over house wrap, with flashed J-channel.

  6. GuttersSeamless, cut on site.

    Seamless aluminum gutters, cut to length on site. Gutter guards, and gutter cleaning.
