# Rembrandt Roofing & Restoration: mood (R7)

Reference file for critique-brand-consistency ⟦S1496⟧. Written from the locked facts and the owner's verdict, not invented.

## Personality
- **Steady.** A family firm since 1995 (Franklin "Bunk" Walker; his son Wade president since 2015). Nothing shouts.
- **Plain-spoken and checkable.** Every claim can be checked (4.9 stars from 295 Google reviews, BBB A+ accredited since 2001,
  Owens Corning Platinum Preferred, $2M liability insurance).
- **A person answers.** Rachel answers the phone, Monday to Friday, 9 to 5. The site's job is to get that call.

## Aesthetic direction
- **Calm, light, restful.** The owner's verdict on R4 was that the colours "hurt his eyes". Grounds are off-white from the
  tinted neutral ramp; most of every page rests in neutrals; the one accent appears with discipline.
- **Two brand colours, one job each** (R7, the owner's approved Figma colours). Deep green #0e3b2c is the primary: the one
  solid action, links, selected states and the deep-green brand bands. Lime #b5db6e is the highlight: one pop per view,
  a drawing's focal mark, a data highlight, a marker. Green reads as roofs over lawns and trees, a trade that works
  outdoors; lime is the fresh-growth note that keeps the deep green from turning sombre.
- **Real photography carries the colour.** The job photos are full of green lawns, blue sky and red brick; the interface
  stays quiet around them.
- **Drawings are line-and-neutral, with one accent mark.** Houses, roofs and tools are drawn in neutral ink and fills; the
  accent marks one focal part per drawing.

## Do
- One filled action per view, in the accent.
- Neutral headings and labels; accent text only on interactive things.
- A deep-green ground at most once or twice per page (the Call band, a close); it must read as green, never black.
- Lime only as the one pop per view, and only under deep-green or near-black text.

## Don't
- Saturated fields behind body text, or one after another, section after section.
- Accent-coloured words inside headlines.
- Pure black grounds, near-black grounds, or any third hue.
- White text on lime; lime scattered across a view (lime everywhere is lime nowhere).
