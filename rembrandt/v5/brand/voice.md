# Rembrandt Roofing & Restoration: voice (R6)

Reference file for critique-brand-consistency ⟦S1496⟧, from the copy rules already locked on this site (facts lock, no
invented claims) and the S0011 voice framework.

| Trait | We are | We are not |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | "Call Rachel." "Monday to Friday, 9 to 5." | Salesy ("Unleash your dream roof!") |
| Honest | "Your insurance company decides the claim." | Promising outcomes, prices or approval |
| Local | "24 towns around Springboro." | Generic ("serving the area") |
| Family | "Bunk founded it in 1995; his son Wade runs it." | Corporate ("our team of experts") |

- Short sentences, second person, numerals for counts.
- CTA label: "Call (937) 746-7377" or "Call Rachel". One label per intent.
- Prohibited: 24/7 or emergency claims; any licence number; (937) 353-9711; invented reviews, prices or guarantees; filler
  verbs (elevate, seamless, unleash); scroll cues.
