One night in May.

Hail season here runs April to July.

About 19 hail days a year.

That's Ohio's average. Most of them land on roofs nobody looks at again until something drips.

Wind gets under the tabs.

Shingles are nailed at the top. Wind lifts them from the bottom, breaks the seal, and the next rain finds the gap.

Most roof damage doesn't show from the street.

Bruised shingles look fine from the driveway. They lose their granules over the next year or two.

Then, one morning, the ceiling.

By the time water shows inside, it has usually been getting in for a while.

    Plain answers

    How do I know if I have hail damage?

    From the ground you usually can't. Look for granules in the gutters, dents on the gutters or downspouts, and dings on a grill or car from the same storm. The roof itself needs a look in low light. We do that for free.

    Answered by the Rembrandt office

    Will my insurance pay?

    It depends on your policy and on whether the damage came from a covered storm. We can't promise a result, but we document the damage properly and meet your adjuster with the photos if you want us there.

    Answered by the Rembrandt office

    How long does a roof replacement take?

    Most homes are one day, two for large or steep roofs. The roof is dried in before the crew leaves each day.

    Answered by the Rembrandt office

    Do I need to be home?

    Not for the work. We like you there for the final walkthrough, because that's when we show you the photos and you sign off.

    Answered by the Rembrandt office

    What does the warranty cover?

    Our workmanship, for life, from the day you sign off. The Owens Corning materials carry 50 years of non-prorated coverage on a full system. Storm damage after the job is an insurance matter, not a warranty one.

    Answered by the Rembrandt office

    Do you do repairs, or only replacements?

    Both. We have a dedicated repair crew, and if a repair is enough, that's what we'll recommend.

    Answered by the Rembrandt office

    What areas do you serve?

    Springboro and 23 towns around Dayton, roughly 25 miles out. Check your ZIP above.
