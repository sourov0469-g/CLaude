Scoring note: scores are 0–5 per criterion, total /35. Animation and Mobile are judged from the text-fetch impression — text fetches strip hero/nav and JS animation, and JS counters often render as 0 — so Animation is scored conservatively for every site. ### Segment patterns worth stealing for GC templates
1. Licence number in the header/footer chrome, not buried on an About page (CQC Home; Renuity's 30-state footer; Murray Lampert's CSLB-linked number).
2. A named, staged process with a price-certainty step in the middle (DFW Improved's Fixed Price Agreement; Murray Lampert's "price settled before build"; FBC's 12-step Build Joy).
3. Performance stats instead of adjectives (Lars: 94% on-budget, 6% change orders vs ~20% industry, written on-time guarantee).
4. Reviews broken out by platform with counts (Lars; Neil Kelly's 4.6/5 from 2,200+ reviews).
5. Low-friction first step under the main CTA (CQC's 15-minute Book A Call; MOSS's virtual discovery session; Cipriani's "10–15 minute call, no home visit, no obligation").
