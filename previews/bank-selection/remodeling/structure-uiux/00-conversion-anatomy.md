- Homepage with process CLAIMED but not shown, and no reviews (Meadowlark homepage — press substitutes for proof; OPENED).
- Placeholder testimonials left live, literally labelled "Placeholder reviews" (Mozy Construction, GC scan — OPENED by Lane 5 segment).
- Placeholder HEADING left live — "Request Lorem Epsom" as a form heading on the homepage of a NARI Regional Remodeler of the Year winner (Honey Do Men, GC Wave 2 — OPENED), alongside a Why-Choose-Us block duplicated ×3 and stock nature photos (ocean/river/dunes) inside project sliders: award-winning work, unmaintained website.

- Stock photos in a portfolio (Shoal Signature Homes — Unsplash URLs visible in fetch).
