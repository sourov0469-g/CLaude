## The trade splits in three — pick the machine before the design
1. **One-day bath / productised remodel:** wins on offer clarity — 1-day install, lifetime warranty, named discount, financing in the hero. Models: Bath Fitter, Re-Bath, West Shore (excerpt).
2. **Design-build (the Asiful template model):** wins on process clarity + proof depth — named process with a price mechanism inside it, case-study portfolio, consultation CTA. Models: Elbaz, CMK, DreamMaker, CRD (excerpt), BOWA (excerpt).
3. **Specialist (ADU / basement / aging / historic):** wins on niche authority + published numbers — CAPS/VA fluency, all-in model pricing, penalty-backed promises. Models: Harmony Basements, Abodu, DFW Improved, Fred Beese.
Mixing machines dilutes all three: discount sites with design-studio language, design-build sites with no price mechanism, generalists pretending to specialise.
