designed in Sweden

TDoS embodies the legacy of Swedish design - where innovation, sustainability, and timeless aesthetics converge. "Designed in Sweden" is more than a statement; it reflects a heritage of precision, functionality, and refined simplicity. For over 60 years, TDoS has shaped the industry of cruise ships, crafting transformative experiences that redefine luxury and set new standards for sustainable elegance at sea.

shaping icons

 

Icons that redefine the seas. Through visionary design and timeless elegance, TDoS transforms vessels into experiences, crafted for those who seek more than travel. It celebrates every day the shared spirit between legendary brands and groundbreaking design, where every line drawn echoes a story of innovation. Shaping Icons is an invitation to explore, to feel, and to remember what makes something truly iconic.

cruise ship

By merging timeless elegance with forward-thinking solutions, TDoS creates spaces that welcome, inspire and connect. Sixty years of experience guide every decision, ensuring that each new vessel carries both heritage and vision towards the horizon.

cruise yacht

TDoS’s experience in cruise design led the studio to rethink the model itself, questioning how the scale and efficiency of a cruise vessel could coexist with the intimacy of a private yacht. The cruise yacht concept is its answer and a sector it has helped bring to life.

 

Discover

 

river cruise ship

TDoS’s history in ship design has shaped a clear understanding that river cruising is not about scale, but about connection. For TDoS, the route is the starting point of every river vessel. Behind the scenes, multidisciplinary teams ensure structural integrity and operational clarity, allowing the guest experience to feel effortless and authentic.

expedition cruise

Expedition cruising is about access, and access depends on design. With years of experience in complex shipbuilding, TDoS creates vessels where advanced operational needs are met seamlessly, without compromising comfort.

sustainability

 

 

 

For TDoS, sustainability is not just a practice; it is the foundation of every design. It goes beyond materials and processes, embodying a philosophy that guides every decision. Each year, TDoS reflects on its environmental footprint and employee wellbeing, continuously seeking growth and improvement in harmony with nature.
