**Scope:** 109 roofing companies logged in `lane2-scan.csv`; **107 opened and judged** (2 dead: PetersenDean — domain hijacked post-bankruptcy; Crown Roofing — defunct). Sources: 4 scan chunks (US-South, UK/ROW, parent opens, curl/Wayback retries) + source-level (F12) mining of the top tier in `lane2-source-findings.md`.
**Method honesty:** scores are 0–5 per dimension (Design craft / Animation / Proof & trust / CTA & conversion / Mobile / Speed-feel / Trade fit; /35). Animation and Mobile are text+source-proxy inferences for chunk-scored sites, measured from source only for the deep 5. A high-tech agency look is NOT credited as trade fit — these are judged as a homeowner on a phone judges them: real work, proof everywhere, clear offer, phone/quote path in seconds.
**How to use this file:** the shortlist is the menu; the deep 5 are the rebuild-grade teardowns; the TOP 1% crown + template synthesis at the end is what goes into Asiful's roofing template.

## Template synthesis — the roofing template steal-stack
1. **Hero:** proof strip ABOVE headline (★rating · count · licence no. · since year) → one trademark-style claim or honesty headline → funnel step 1 or Call/WhatsApp with equal weight.
2. **Offer:** a NAMED free thing with a dollar value and a number (Free Roof Plan™ / 72-point inspection), priced in writing.
3. **Trust:** licence linked to the official record + Verify links; review count in the page title; JSON-LD AggregateRating so stars show in Google.
4. **Honesty block:** "If you don't need a new roof, we'll tell you — in writing" + why-we're-cheaper/candour FAQ.
5. **Local proof:** real job photos captioned by town/postcode; process-story sequence; a current review feed, never a static screenshot of reviews.
6. **Fear-killer:** local firm-death/longevity stat where true ("the ten-year question") or insurance-help proven by reviews in storm areas.
7. **Motion budget (from Stronghold's CSS):** reveals .15–.28s ease-out, one slow marquee (60s) for projects, drag slider for before/after — nothing that delays the phone path.
8. **Plumbing:** CallRail-style call tracking + first/last-touch attribution (Green Conception's gc-lead.js pattern) feeding the CRM.
