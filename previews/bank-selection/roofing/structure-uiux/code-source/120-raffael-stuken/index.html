I’m a graphic designer and developer, based in Cologne. Currently serving as creative lead and partner at Humans & Machines, a design and technology studio located in Berlin.

In the decade leading up to Humans & Machines, I focused on establishing my own studio practice. During this time, I primarily worked with clients in the cultural sector, including institutions such as Schauspielhaus Bochum and Klavierfestival Ruhr.

Later on, I transitioned into freelancing for international agencies, undertaking a diverse array of projects for clients including Kanye West and Virgil Abloh, Stella McCartney, and Garage Magazine.

In 2012, I co-founded Discover Shadow, an app for dream tracking. It garnered attention from TED, The New Yorker, and Netflix, among others. In 2015, I became part of the founding team at Colido, focusing on developing a SAAS platform for collection management.

Initially rooted in editorial and print design, as time progressed I increasingly gravitated towards the digital landscape. At Humans & Machines, we focus on creating bespoke digital products that scale and last.

Colophon

Objective

After launching my first digital portfolio in 2005 and undergoing a few rebuilds, I haven’t had a personal website for quite some time. However, despite the absence of a necessity, I felt the urge to create something new—a virtual playground that is essentially a personal sanctuary for my own exploration.

Approach

I dived into the idea of creating a lo-fi, scroll-driven experience solely utilizing semantic HTML and modern CSS. Harnessing the capabilities of features like CSS grid, sticky positioning, and mask-image. Eventually shipping less than two kilobytes of JavaScript.
