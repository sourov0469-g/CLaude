Roofing questions North Atlanta homeowners ask before scheduling

Will my homeowner’s insurance cover my roof replacement?

If your roof is 8 years or older and your home has been impacted by a major storm within the past year, you may qualify for an insurance-covered roof replacement. Many Roswell and North Atlanta homeowners meet these criteria but do not realize damage may already be present. Bonner Built performs a thorough inspection, documents qualifying damage, and guides you through the claims process from start to finish so you never have to navigate it alone.

What makes Bonner Built different from other roofing companies?

Bonner Built is builder-led, not sales-driven. Our licensed general contractor oversight brings deeper structural expertise to every project, ensuring your roofing system protects your home long term, not just temporarily. As a family-owned company, we focus on quality, accountability, and relationships rather than volume.

Does Bonner Built offer a warranty on your work?

Yes. Every project includes manufacturer warranty coverage on materials, plus Bonner Built’s own workmanship warranty on the installation itself. Coverage details vary depending on the service and materials involved, and we’ll walk you through exactly what’s included before any work begins, so you know what’s protected long after the job is done.

How much does a roof replacement cost in North Atlanta?

Roof replacement costs vary depending on size, materials, and damage. When insurance is involved, many homeowners pay far less than expected. Bonner Built provides clear, detailed estimates so you understand exactly what your project includes before work begins.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Many residential roof replacements can be completed in a day, but timelines depend on roof size, complexity, weather, and any underlying repairs. Your project timeline will be confirmed after inspection and planning.

How do I know if my roof has storm damage?

Storm damage isn’t always visible from the ground. Common signs include missing or lifted shingles, hail impact marks, granule loss, damaged flashing, dented gutters or vents, and new leaks or water stains inside your home. After a severe thunderstorm, hailstorm, or high winds in the North Atlanta area, it’s a good idea to have your roof professionally inspected, even if everything looks normal from the ground. Bonner Built provides professional roof inspections to identify storm damage and help you understand whether repairs or replacement may be needed.

Why does builder-led experience matter for roofing?

Your roof is a structural system that protects everything underneath it. Builder-led oversight ensures proper installation, ventilation, flashing, and long-term durability. This level of expertise helps prevent costly issues down the road.
