Home Care Treatment: Our 4-Step Process

DIAGNOSE

During our free in-home inspection, we will check everything on the exterior of your Atlanta home to diagnose any problems with your home's roof, siding, and windows.

PRESCRIBE

Every home is unique, and so is the solution it needs. Our certified Atlanta exterior remodeling experts will create a prescription plan unique to your roofing, siding, or window project. During your free consultation, you'll hear about our 18-point proprietary LeakBlock program. We NEVER re-use materials, which is the number one cause of future roof leaks.

TREAT

We take our time and don't rush your home improvement project. Like all trusted professionals, we "measure twice and cut once." We will never try to convince you to finish a roof replacement project in just one day. We always use only Dr. Roof-certified crews in which there is always a dedicated independent quality control expert overseeing your project. When finished, your roofing, siding, or windows won't be just better, they will be the best they can be.

BILL OF HEALTH

The work on your home is guaranteed forever with our LifeRoof Program. Ask about our extensive warranties! Once your roofing project is completed, you become a member of our exclusive Dr. Roof Concierge Program, with lifetime benefits.
