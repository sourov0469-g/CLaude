## Methodology
- Sources: candidate list from lane brief, national roundups (Modernize, BestCompany, EcoWatch — LEADS ONLY), agency design roundups (CyberOptik siding list, Hook Agency — LEADS ONLY), metro searches (TX, FL, Chicago, Seattle, Portland, MD).
- Every scored site was actually opened with browser.open (text fetch) and judged from its own pages — never from a roundup description. Scan log: `lane4-siding-scan.csv`.
- Scoring 0–5 each: Design craft / Animation / Proof & trust / CTA & conversion / Mobile (inferred from structure/text order, not device-tested) / Speed-feel (page-weight signals in fetched content) / Trade fit. Total /35.
- Evidence labels: **source-verified** = found in page HTML by the lead's curl source pass (see SOURCE PASS at end) · **excerpt** = visible in browser.open text fetch · **not-detected** = looked for, not found in the evidence available. Animation scores are conservative: text fetch strips most JS/CSS, so motion is scored from evidenced mechanisms (video, counters, sliders, visualizers), not measured durations. Where animation "parameters" appear below they are the mechanism + the design-bank standard build range, labelled as such — never presented as measured from the site.
- Manufacturers (James Hardie, Pella, Andersen parent, Marvin, ProVia, Milgard, Alside) are scored but flagged: their job is dealer routing and brand desire, not local lead capture. Trade-fit is scored down for that, deliberately.
