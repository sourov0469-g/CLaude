Kansas City’s Trusted HVAC, Plumbing, Sewer, Drain, Electrical & Appliance Experts

Enjoy Consistently Great Service With Every Call

For more than 65 years, A.B. May Heating, A/C, Plumbing & Electrical has been a trusted part of the Kansas City community. Founded in 1959 and built through three generations of family ownership, we’re proud to continue delivering the dependable home services Kansas City has counted on for decades.

From routine maintenance to unexpected repairs, we’re committed to delivering expert workmanship, honest recommendations, and a job done right—giving homeowners confidence and peace of mind every step of the way.

Our highly trained technicians are available with same-day service and 24/7 emergency support, so you don’t have to wait when you need heating, cooling, plumbing, electrical, or appliance service.

Heating Services

Reliable indoor comfort is a necessity in the harsh Kansas and Missouri winters. When you need fast heating services for your boiler, heat pump, furnace, or geothermal system, turn to A.B. May Heating, A/C, Plumbing & Electrical. We provide:

  • Heating repair
  • Heating installation and replacement
  • Heating maintenance

Air Conditioning Services

Our team of NATE-certified technicians delivers the expert AC services your home needs when you need them, including:

  • Air conditioning repair
  • Air conditioning installation and replacement
  • Air conditioning maintenance

Whether you rely on central AC or ductless mini-splits, we can help. We also offer indoor air quality solutions to ensure the conditioned air from your vents is free of airborne contaminants.

Plumbing Services

At A.B. May Heating, A/C, Plumbing & Electrical, our plumbers can resolve any plumbing problem, whether it’s large or small.

If you have a leaking tank or diminished hot water capacity, we can provide water heater repair or replacement for storage tank and tankless units.

If you notice gurgling toilets or suspect a broken sewer main, we’ll complete a camera inspection to determine if you need trench or trenchless sewer repair or replacement.

Drain Services

Don’t ignore clogged drains or try to clear them yourself with harsh chemical cleaners. A.B. May Heating, A/C, Plumbing & Electrical provides professional drain cleaning and clearing and hydro jetting.

Whether it’s your kitchen or bathroom sink drain, shower drain, or main drain, we’ll locate the blockage and clear any buildup to ensure free-flowing drains.

Electrical Services

We care about your family’s safety, so we offer electrical safety evaluations to help identify and mitigate the risk of electrical fire hazards.

Our licensed electricians also provide:

  • Electrical panel upgrades
  • Rewiring
  • Electric vehicle charging
  • And more

Generator Installation

Power outages can strike anytime, leaving your home without essential systems like heating, cooling, lighting, and refrigeration. Installing a Generac whole-home generator ensures your household stays powered and protected during unexpected blackouts. These reliable systems automatically restore electricity within seconds, helping to maintain comfort, safety, and peace of mind.

With expert installation from A.B. May Heating, A/C, Plumbing & Electrical, you get trusted guidance, professional service, and long-term support from a team committed to powering your home with dependable energy solutions.

Appliance Services

Your appliances need professional service, just like the other systems your home relies on. A.B. May Heating, A/C, Plumbing & Electrical provides appliance wiring and repair for:

  • Washing machines
  • Refrigerators
  • Dishwashers
  • And more
