A/C Repair

When it’s hot out, the last thing you want is to deal with a broken air conditioner. At A/C Authority in Port St. Lucie, we’ll come out with a trained HVAC tech, diagnose the problem and quickly fix your air conditioning unit restoring comfort to your space. A/C Maintenance

Our highly qualified technicians will assess the condition of your A/C system by examining physical components such as electrical motors, relays, capacitors, contactors, and drain lines. They will also determine the efficiency components such as refrigerant levels and properties, fan speeds, duct conditions, and insulation. Lastly, to maintain indoor air quality, they will examine the system for any biological growth or soil
