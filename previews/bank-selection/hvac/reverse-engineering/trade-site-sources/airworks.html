California's updated Title 24 building code took effect January 1, 2026, making heat pumps the prescriptive default for new construction and strongly incentivizing them for system replacements. Qualifying Ventura County homeowners can combine this with HEEHRA rebates of up to $8,000 for high-efficiency heat pump upgrades. AirWorks Heating, Air & Plumbing guides homeowners through the requirements and handles rebate-eligible installations.

AC replacement cost depends on system size, efficiency rating, and ductwork condition; 2026 California market data puts the average heat pump installation around $10,250. AirWorks Heating, Air & Plumbing provides free written, flat-rate estimates — and will review any competitor quote for free, so you never overpay for equipment or work you don't need.

Santa Ana winds push fine dust and wildfire particles into homes through leaky ducts, gaps in insulation, and unsealed attic bypasses. Sealing ductwork, upgrading attic insulation, and adding whole-home air filtration stops most infiltration. AirWorks Heating, Air & Plumbing offers 'Mom-Approved' insulation and indoor air quality solutions designed for Ventura County's wind and wildfire seasons.

For most California homes, yes: a tankless water heater heats water only when you use it instead of keeping a 40–50 gallon tank hot around the clock, which lowers gas usage and frees up space. SoCal Gas rebate programs can further offset installation cost. AirWorks Heating, Air & Plumbing installs and services tank and tankless systems across Ventura County.
