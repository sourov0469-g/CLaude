## Template synthesis — the HVAC template steal-stack (TX/FL heat market)
1. **Hero:** priced offer first ($79 diagnostic or the client's real equivalent) + licence no. + ★/count + since-year in one strip → symptom picker ("What's going on?") as the form's first field → phone at hero weight, after-hours number separate.
2. **Response deadline with money or minutes on it:** call back within 30 min (Fuse) / within the hour (Strike Force) / $5 a minute late (Fallon) — never "fast response."
3. **Guarantees, named:** a buyback or a no-commission promise or an on-time-or-free trio — named like products, printed on every ticket/page.
4. **Price transparency device:** published ranges in the FAQ (Cooling Co) or a no-phone-required estimator/quiz (Expo) — the form is the last step, not the first ask.
5. **Proof placement discipline:** the count goes in the hero (One Hour), split by location when multi-site (Expo), schema'd in JSON-LD (A/C Authority 4.9/387), and CONSISTENT everywhere — one number, one source (the Fuse lesson).
6. **Plan with published pricing:** tiers printed (A.B. May Bronze $14.95 / Silver $70 / Gold $80) or member count published (Deljo 3,800+) — a plan is a retention engine and proof in one.
7. **Photos:** real jobs shot by techs, captioned as such; founder story that makes the promises personal.
8. **Motion budget:** the lane has NO animation craft — the template's edge is Evergreen-grade clarity + .15–.28s reveals (the Stronghold dosage) + a video hero only if it's real techs and stream-delivered. In an emergency market, speed-feel IS the design.
